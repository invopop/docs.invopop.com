A company can change Plateforme Agréée (PA) at any time. The new PA sends a `MOBILITY_REQUEST` to the old one, and the old PA answers each address with a `MOBILITY_RESPONSE`: accepted, accepted on another date, or refused. Invopop handles both sides of this exchange:

- **Incoming:** another PA asks to take over an address registered through Invopop.
- **Outgoing:** you register an address another PA holds.

## Incoming requests

### Setup

In the **Apps** view, open the **France** app, click **Configure**, and select the workflow under **Migration Workflow**. Without one, requests for the workspace's parties are left for Invopop support to answer by hand.

When another PA asks to take over addresses registered through Invopop, Invopop starts one migration job per requested address. Each job runs the configured workflow on the silo entry that registered the address.

Invopop only accepts a request when it comes from the requesting platform's registered contact address. Anything else is refused and no job starts.

### Takeover form

The **Start migration** step publishes a `link_url` under the `migration-link` key in the silo entry meta. Each silo entry has one link, which always opens the latest request for its address.

<Accordion title="Example migration-link meta entry">
```json
{
  "src": "gov-fr",
  "key": "migration-link",
  "ref": "Xk3pQ9vLm2A",
  "link_url": "https://gouv-fr.invopop.com/mig/Xk3pQ9vLm2A/start",
  "link_scope": "public",
  "indexed": true,
  "shared": true
}
```
</Accordion>

The form, in French, shows the requesting platform and the address, and lets the company:

- **Accept** the takeover, on the requested date or on another effective date picked from a calendar. The calendar only offers dates from the earliest the switch can happen: at least 3 business days and 4 calendar days from the answer.
- **Refuse** the takeover.

### Answer deadline

The company has **5 business days** from the request's arrival to answer. This deadline only applies to workspaces with a migration workflow configured (see [Setup](#setup)). The form shows the deadline. If the address is still unanswered after that, **Wait for migration approval** accepts it automatically, as the AIFE process requires: on the requested date when that is still possible, otherwise on the earliest possible date.

### Response

**Complete migration** sends the `MOBILITY_RESPONSE` for the address to the new PA:

| Answer | Response status |
|---|---|
| Accepted on the requested date | `ACCP` |
| Accepted on another date | `ACWC`, with the agreed effective date |
| Refused | `RJCT`, with motif `REFUS_CLIENT` |

The request is complete once every address in it has been answered.

<Note>
  If **Start Peppol migration** ran, the response carries its Peppol migration key so the new PA can move the participant on the Peppol network. Otherwise the key field is `NEANT`.
</Note>

## Outgoing takeovers

When you run the [PPF register supplier](/guides/fr-pa-registration#ppf-register-supplier-workflow) workflow for an address another PA holds, **Register party in Directory** (`gov-fr.directory.register`) sends a `MOBILITY_REQUEST` to that PA and waits for its answer. Once the takeover is accepted, Invopop registers the line with the agreed effective date, and the step stays queued until that date.

If the other PA refuses, or its answer can't be processed, the step fails with the reason.
