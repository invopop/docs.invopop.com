## Introduction

Since 1 January 2025, every German business must be able to receive structured e-invoices: XRechnung, or ZUGFeRD with its embedded XML. Invopop receives them by email with the [Email](/apps/email) app, or through the [Peppol](/apps/peppol) network, and imports each one as a GOBL invoice.

For issuing invoices, see the companion guide: [Germany issuing invoices guide](/guides/de-invoicing).

## Prerequisites

- A registered supplier. Follow the [supplier registration guide](/guides/de-registration) first.
- **For email**: the [Email](/apps/email) app, connected in your workspace.
- **For Peppol**: the [OASIS UBL](/apps/oasis-ubl) and [UN/CEFACT CII](/apps/uncefact-cii) apps, connected in your workspace. They convert the received invoices.

## Receive by email

The Email app gives each registered supplier an address on `invopop.email`, and imports every XRechnung or ZUGFeRD invoice sent there, with the supplier as the customer. [Receiving invoices by email](/guides/email#receiving-invoices-by-email) in the Email guide explains each step in detail.

All of the following steps must be carried out from the [Invopop Console](https://console.invopop.com).

<Steps>
  <Step title="Create the import workflow">
    <Card iconType="duotone" title="Email receive invoice workflow" icon="code-branch" href="https://console.invopop.com/redirect/workflows/new?template=email-incoming-import" horizontal>
      Add to my workspace →
    </Card>
  </Step>
  <Step title="Configure receiving in the Email app">
    In **Configuration** → **Apps**, open the Email app's **Configure** page. In the **Incoming** section, add a connection with Invopop as the provider and the import workflow from the previous step.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Register the supplier for email">
    Run this workflow on the supplier. It gives the supplier its `invopop.email` address. A registered supplier costs 20 pops a month.

    <Card iconType="duotone" title="Email register party workflow" icon="code-branch" href="https://console.invopop.com/redirect/workflows/new?template=email-incoming-register" horizontal>
      Add to my workspace →
    </Card>
  </Step>
  <Step title="Share the address">
    Give the address to the companies that send the supplier its invoices. The Email app lists it in its **Endpoints** tab.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Receive through Peppol

The Peppol app receives the invoices sent to the supplier's Peppol ID, and imports each one as a GOBL invoice. [Receiving invoices](/guides/peppol#receiving-invoices) in the Peppol guide explains each step in detail.

All of the following steps must be carried out from the [Invopop Console](https://console.invopop.com).

<Steps>
  <Step title="Create the receive workflow">
    <Card iconType="duotone" title="Peppol receive document workflow" icon="code-branch" href="https://console.invopop.com/redirect/workflows/new?template=peppol-receive" horizontal>
      Add to my workspace →
    </Card>
  </Step>
  <Step title="Configure the Peppol app">
    In **Configuration** → **Apps**, open the Peppol app's **Configure** page. Set **Incoming Workflow** to the receive workflow from the previous step.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Share the Peppol ID">
    Give the supplier's Peppol ID to the companies that send it invoices. A supplier without a Peppol ID gets one from its VAT number at registration, such as `0246:DE123456789`.
  </Step>
</Steps>
