## Introduction

Since 1 January 2025, every German business must be able to receive structured e-invoices: XRechnung, or ZUGFeRD with its embedded XML. Invopop receives them by email with the [Connect](/apps/connect) app, or through the [Peppol](/apps/peppol) network, and imports each one as a GOBL invoice.

For issuing invoices, see the companion guide: [Invoicing in Germany](/guides/de-invoicing).

## Prerequisites

- **For email**: an address that your suppliers send invoices to. Connect gives you an `invopop.email` address, or receives on your own domain.
- **For Peppol**: a registered supplier. Follow the [supplier registration guide](/guides/de-registration) first.

## Receive through Peppol

Register the supplier with the [supplier registration guide](/guides/de-registration). Then follow [Receiving invoices](/guides/peppol#receiving-invoices) in the Peppol guide: the Peppol app imports each invoice with the workflow that you set as its incoming workflow.
