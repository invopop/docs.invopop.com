## Introduction

Brazil has a variety of electronic tax documents that must be issued in different cases for various tax purposes. These include service invoices (NFS-e), product invoices (NF-e), and consumer invoices (NFC-e). Each document type is subject to variations from state to state and municipality to municipality, with each jurisdiction applying its own rules, formats, and APIs for issuing fiscal documents.

Invopop's **Documentos Fiscais Eletrônicos** app provides a unified way to issue fiscal documents across all Brazilian states and over 2000 municipalities using GOBL. You can find [the full list of supported municipalities here](https://tecnospeed.com.br/plugdfe/nfse/#homologacao).

For onboarding suppliers, see the companion guide: [DF-e supplier registration](/guides/br-dfe-supplier).

## Which document for which flow

- **AR, accounts receivable:** documents the supplier issues to its customers.
- **AP, accounts payable:** documents the supplier receives from its own suppliers.

| Flow | Buyer | Operation | Document |
|---|---|---|---|
| AR | B2C | Goods sold in person, or with home delivery in the same state | [NFC-e](/guides/br-dfe-nfce), model 65 |
| AR | B2C | Goods sold online, or delivered to another state | [NF-e](/guides/br-dfe-nfe), model 55 |
| AR | B2B | Goods sold in Brazil | [NF-e](/guides/br-dfe-nfe), model 55 |
| AR | B2B or B2C | Goods sold to a foreign customer | [NF-e](/guides/br-dfe-nfe), model 55 |
| AR | B2B or B2C | Goods returned by the customer | [NF-e](/guides/br-dfe-nfe#return-of-an-nfc-e), model 55, entry document |
| AR | B2B or B2C | Services | [NFS-e](/guides/br-dfe-nfse) |
| AP | - | Documents received from suppliers | Not covered by these guides |

An NFC-e only takes the in-person or home delivery presence codes ([`GOBL-BR-NFE-BILL-INVOICE-28`](https://docs.gobl.org/addons/br-nfe-v4#validation-rules)). Any other consumer sale of goods is an NF-e.

## How documents are checked

Each document goes through three checks, in this order:

| Step | Who checks | When it fails | Runs in sandbox |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1. Upload the entry | GOBL, with the [`br-nfe-v4`](https://docs.gobl.org/addons/br-nfe-v4) or [`br-nfse-v1`](https://docs.gobl.org/addons/br-nfse-v1) add-on rules | The upload returns HTTP 422 with a `GOBL-BR-…` code | Yes |
| 2. Run the issue workflow | Invopop and its partner PlugNotas build the XML and sign it with the supplier's A1 certificate | The job fails | No, the sandbox returns mock files |
| 3. Authorization | SEFAZ for NF-e and NFC-e. The municipality for NFS-e | The job fails with the authority's rejection | No |

<Warning>
The sandbox mocks steps 2 and 3. A document that passes in the sandbox can still fail in homologação. Before going live, check the [SEFAZ checks not run by GOBL](/guides/br-dfe-nfce#sefaz-checks-not-run-by-gobl).
</Warning>

## Environments

| - | Sandbox | Live: homologação | Live: produção |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Workspace** | Sandbox | Live | Live |
| **Responses** | Simulated/mocked | From the tax authority's test environment | From the tax authority |
| **PDF & XML** | Always the same mock-up files attached to silo entry | Actual XML and PDF, no fiscal validity | Actual XML sent to tax authority and actual generated PDF attached |
| **Chosen** | Always simulation mode | When the supplier is registered | When the supplier is registered |

<Warning>
In live workspaces, documents are sent to the environment — homologation or production — that the supplier is currently registered in. Documents sent to homologation are processed by the tax authority's test environment and have no fiscal validity. See [Switch between environments](/guides/br-dfe-supplier#switch-between-environments) for how to check and change a supplier's environment.
</Warning>
