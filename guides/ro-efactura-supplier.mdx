## Introduction

[RO e-Factura](https://mfinante.gov.ro/ro/web/efactura) is Romania's mandatory national e-invoicing system, run by the Ministry of Finance and [ANAF](https://www.anaf.ro), the Romanian tax authority. Before you can send or receive invoices through RO e-Factura on behalf of a party (supplier), the party must authorize Invopop with ANAF.

Authorization uses ANAF's OAuth flow. Invopop generates a link, and the person holding the company's SPV (_Spațiul Privat Virtual_) digital certificate opens it, signs in on ANAF's site with the certificate and approves the access. The certificate never leaves its owner: Invopop only receives the access ANAF grants, valid for one year.

In this guide you'll learn how to register a party using the [Romania app](/apps/romania). Once a party is registered, continue with the companion guides: [Romania: Issuing invoices](/guides/ro-efactura) and [Romania: Receiving invoices](/guides/ro-efactura-receiving).

| - | Sandbox | Live |
|---|---|---|
| **Party** | Real Romanian CIF and SPV digital certificate | Real Romanian CIF and SPV digital certificate |
| **Environment** | ANAF test environment, documents have no fiscal value | ANAF production environment |

## Prerequisites

To register a party with RO e-Factura, you will need:

- **The party's Romanian tax ID (CIF)**, entered as the party's `tax_id` with country `RO`.
- **A qualified digital certificate** of a person registered in SPV as legal representative or authorized person of the company, installed in the browser used to open the registration link.
- **The Cron app**, if you want to receive invoices. The registration workflow subscribes the party to the periodic sync of received invoices.

<Note>
  ANAF signs in with the same certificate in both environments, so even sandbox registrations need a real certificate with SPV access. Only the invoices are kept apart: in sandbox, they go to ANAF's test environment.
</Note>

## Setup

All of the following steps must be carried out from the [Invopop Console](https://console.invopop.com).

<Steps>

<Step title="Connect the Romania app">
  1. Navigate to **Configuration** → **Apps**
  2. Find **Romania** in the app discovery list
  3. Click **Connect** to activate

<Frame>
  <img width="372" src="/assets/guides/ro-efactura-connect.png" alt="Connect the Romania app" />
</Frame>

The initial connection requires no configuration.
</Step>

<Step title="Connect the Cron app">
  1. Navigate to **Configuration** → **Apps**
  2. Find **Cron** in the app discovery list
  3. Click **Connect** to activate

The [Cron app](/apps/cron) runs the sync of received invoices at the interval you choose.

<Info>You can skip this step if you don't plan to receive invoices.</Info>
</Step>

<Step title="Configure the party registration workflow">

This workflow registers a party with RO e-Factura, then subscribes it to the periodic sync of received invoices:

1. **Set state** to `Processing`.
2. **Register with ANAF** generates the ANAF authorization link and stores it in the party's **Meta** section.
3. **Wait ANAF link registration** pauses the workflow until the certificate holder completes the authorization.
4. **Set state** to `Registered`.
5. **Subscribe to periodic execution** subscribes the party to the sync workflow through the Cron app.

The **Subscribe to periodic execution** step needs the **RO Sync Received Invoices** workflow, created in the [receiving invoices guide](/guides/ro-efactura-receiving#setup). Select it in the step, along with how often the sync should run.

<Info>
  If you only want to send invoices, remove the **Subscribe to periodic execution** step. You don't need the Cron app or the sync workflow then.
</Info>

<Warning>
  Not implemented yet. Notifications before an ANAF authorization expires are in development and will soon be supported through the app configuration. The workflow below shows how they will work.

ANAF authorizations last one year, after which the party has to complete the registration again. This workflow checks the party's authorization and continues on one of three branches:

- **ok**: the authorization is valid, nothing to do.
- **warning**: the authorization expires soon. The template sends you an email with the **Notify via email** step.
- **expired**: the authorization has expired and sending or receiving fails until the party registers again. The template sends you an email.

The step also returns the expiry date as the `refresh_token_deadline` argument (`DD/MM/YYYY`), which you can use in the email.

## Register a supplier

<Steps>

<Step title="Create the party">

In the [Invopop Console](https://console.invopop.com), navigate to **Parties** → **Suppliers** and click <kbd>+ New Supplier</kbd>.

Use the company's Romanian CIF and address. The county goes in `state` as its ISO 3166-2:RO code (`B` for Bucharest) and, in Bucharest, the sector goes in `locality`:

<Supplier />

Click <kbd>Build</kbd>, ensure there are no errors, and click <kbd>Save</kbd>.

</Step>

<Step title="Run the workflow">

In the party entry, click <kbd>Select Workflow</kbd>, choose your **RO register party** workflow, and click <kbd>Run Workflow</kbd>.

The party moves to <Badge color="yellow">Processing</Badge> and the job stops at the **Wait ANAF link registration** step until the authorization is complete.

<Frame>
  <img width="390" src="/assets/guides/ro-efactura-wait-step.png" alt="Registration job waiting for the ANAF authorization" />
</Frame>

The **Wait ANAF link registration** step waits indefinitely. It continues as soon as the authorization on ANAF's site ends with the **Registration Successful** page.

- **To cancel the registration**, cancel the job.
- **To restart the registration**, run the **RO register party** workflow again.

<Warning>
  Make sure the job is waiting on the **Wait ANAF link registration** step before completing the authorization on ANAF's site.
</Warning>

</Step>

<Step title="Get the registration link">

<Badge color="green">Your own company</Badge><br />
In Console, go to the party entry → **Meta** tab → click the **link** entry to open ANAF's authorization page.

<Frame>
  <img width="382" src="/assets/guides/ro-efactura-meta-link.png" alt="Party entry Meta tab with the ANAF registration link" />
</Frame>

<Badge color="green">Third-party companies</Badge> (white label)<br />
Add a webhook after the **Register with ANAF** step to receive the `siloEntryId`.

Use the [Fetch an Entry endpoint](/api-ref/silo/entries/fetch-an-entry) to get the registration link from the `meta` object:

```json
{
  "data": {
    "meta": [
      {
        "src": "gov-ro",
        "key": "link",
        "link_url": "https://logincert.anaf.ro/anaf-oauth2/v1/authorize?response_type=code&client_id=...&state=..."
      }
    ]
  }
}
```

Send the `link_url` to the certificate holder.

<Info>
  The link can be opened as many times as needed until the authorization succeeds, for example after choosing the wrong certificate.
</Info>

</Step>

<Step title="Authorize Invopop on ANAF's site">

The certificate holder opens the link in a browser where their digital certificate is installed:

1. The browser asks which certificate to use. Choose the certificate registered in SPV for the company.

   <Frame>
     <img width="600" src="/assets/guides/ro-efactura-certificate-select.png" alt="Browser certificate selection for ANAF" />
   </Frame>

2. ANAF signs in with the certificate and sends the browser straight back to Invopop, which shows the result:

   - ✓ **Registration Successful**: the window can be closed.

     <Frame>
       <img width="600" src="/assets/guides/ro-efactura-registration-success.png" alt="Registration successful page" />
     </Frame>

   - × **Something went wrong**: the page shows the error. Fix the cause and open the same link again.

     <Frame>
       <img width="600" src="/assets/guides/ro-efactura-registration-failure.png" alt="Registration failed page with the error" />
     </Frame>

</Step>

<Step title="Registration completes">

Invopop stores the ANAF authorization and wakes the waiting job up. The registration link is removed from the party's meta, the party moves to <Badge color="purple">Registered</Badge> and, if the workflow includes it, the party is subscribed to the periodic sync of received invoices.

<Frame>
  <img width="383" src="/assets/guides/ro-efactura-registered.png" alt="Party entry in Registered state, with the link removed from meta" />
</Frame>

× **Failure**: see [Why did my party registration fail?](/faq/romania#why-did-my-party-registration-fail) in Romania's FAQ.

</Step>

</Steps>

At this point, you're ready to start sending invoices on behalf of the party. Head over to the [RO e-Factura issuing invoices guide](/guides/ro-efactura) to continue.

## Renew the authorization

ANAF authorizations last one year. To renew one, run the **RO register party** workflow again on the same party entry and complete the authorization with the certificate. The party keeps its history and received invoices continue from where they stopped.

<Note>
  Notifications before an authorization expires are in development and will soon be supported through the app configuration.
</Note>

## Unregister a supplier

Run the **RO unregister party** workflow on the party entry. The party moves to <Badge color="gray">Void</Badge>, the ANAF credentials are deleted from Invopop and the periodic sync stops.

<Warning>
  An unregistered party can no longer send or receive invoices through Invopop. To start again, run the registration workflow and complete the ANAF authorization again.
</Warning>

<Tip>
  To stop receiving invoices without unregistering the party, create a workflow with only the **Unsubscribe from periodic execution** step of the Cron app and run it on the party.
</Tip>
