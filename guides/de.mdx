Since 1 January 2025, every German business must be able to receive structured e-invoices, in the **XRechnung** or **ZUGFeRD** format. Issuing them becomes mandatory from 2027, in stages. Germany has no central platform: invoices travel directly between businesses, by email or through the [Peppol](/apps/peppol) network. Invopop converts GOBL invoices to both formats, and sends and receives them on both channels.

## Choose a channel

Register the supplier first, on both channels. The [Germany](/apps/germany) app approves the supplier, and the Peppol app then registers it on the network.

- **Email**: send invoices with the [Email](/apps/email) app, and receive them with the [Connect](/apps/connect) app.
- **Peppol**: send and receive invoices through the Peppol network. Public authorities receive XRechnung mainly through Peppol.
