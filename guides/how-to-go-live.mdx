Have you set up your sandbox account and everything is working correctly? Time to go live! Here's what you need to do:

### Set up a payment method and live workspace

<Steps>
  <Step title="Access your organization’s settings">
    From the Console, click on your avatar (bottom left) and select **Configure \{organization\_name\}**.

    <Frame caption="Click on 'Admin'">
      <img
        src="/assets/guides/go-live-configure-org.png"
        alt="Configure Organization"
        width="348"
      />
    </Frame>

  </Step>

  <Step title="Create a Billing Account">
  	In the left-hand menu, click on *Billing*, then on *Create Billing Account* (top right). Name your billing account (e.g.: 'Acme Billing') and click on **Save**.
  	 <Frame caption="Create a Billing Account">
      <img
        src="/assets/guides/go-live-create-billing-account.png"
        width="819"
        alt="Create a billing account"
      />
    </Frame>
  </Step>

  <Step title="Add your invoicing information">
    Fill your invoicing information and click on **Save**.
     <Frame caption="Create a Billing Account">
      <img
        width="857"
        src="/assets/guides/go-live-invoicing-information.png"
        alt="Add your invoicing information"
      />
    </Frame>
  </Step>

  <Step title="Add your payment method and billing details">

    Once you add your invoicing information, adding a payment method will be enabled.



		Adding a payment method will take you to the Stripe page:

			* Add a payment method and click *Save*

  	 <Frame caption="Add a card on Stripe">
      <img
        width="466"
        src="/assets/guides/go-live-stripe.png"
        alt="Stripe screenshot"
      />
    </Frame>
  </Step>

  <Step title="Add a subscription plan">

    With the payment method in place, build the subscription for your billing account. A subscription lets you create unlimited workspaces, and is made up of:

    * **Billing period** — pay monthly or yearly.
    * **Pops tier** — the monthly computing credits used to process documents. See [pricing](/get-started/pricing) for what each action costs.
    * **Countries** — one country is included; each additional country adds a flat monthly fee.
    * **Integrations** — third-party platforms such as Stripe or Chargebee, each billed at its own rate.
    * **Pro features** — an optional flat monthly fee that adds chat and email support, unlimited users, audit logs, ISO27001 access, and removes Invopop's watermark from PDFs and emails.

    The estimated total updates as you make your selections. Add a discount code if you have one, then click **Save**.

    <Frame caption="Build your subscription">
      <img
        width="834"
        src="/assets/guides/go-live-subscription.png"
        alt="Billing account subscription form"
      />
    </Frame>
  </Step>


  <Step title="Create your production environment (Live Workspace)">
			Once you introduce your payment details on Stripe you will return to the billing accounts screen. Click on **Workspaces** (in the side menu), then on *New workspace*. Assign a name, select the **Live** type and the billing account you just created. Click *Create workspace*.
  	 <Frame caption="Create your live workspace">
      <img
        width="836"
        src="/assets/guides/go-live-new-workspace.png"
        alt="Create your live workspace"
      />
    </Frame>
  </Step>

</Steps>

#### All set!

Your production environment is now configured. From the Console, you should see your new *Live Workspace* in the selector (top left). If it doesn't appear, reload the page. If the issue persists, contact us at **[support@invopop.com](mailto:support@invopop.com)**.
