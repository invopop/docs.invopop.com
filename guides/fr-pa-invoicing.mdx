The France PA invoicing flow covers regulated B2B exchanges where both parties are registered in the French Directory. All other scenarios are handled by the [e-reporting](/guides/fr-pa-reporting) (flux 10) flow.

This guide walks through how to implement e-invoicing obligations in France: 

* [Workflows](#workflows): sending and receiving workflows for French documents (payments, statuses and invoices).
* [Sending](#sending): recording the invoice, generating the format, transmitting over Peppol, and forwarding the F1 copy to the PPF.
* [Receiving](#receiving): detecting the inbound format, importing it into GOBL, and recording it against your registered party.
* [Invoice formats](#invoice-formats): UBL, CII, and Factur-X and how to choose between them.
* [Self-billing](#self-billing): how *autofacturation* maps onto the same send and receive workflows using the `self-billed` tag.
* [Example invoices](#example-invoices): minimal GOBL documents and their built versions, ready to paste into the GOBL builder.

## Workflows

These workflows are shared between invoice and [status documents](/guides/fr-pa-status). The same workflow processes the different schemas and will perform different actions depending on the document type.  

### Sending

The send workflow records the invoice for compliance, generates the chosen format, transmits it to the receiver’s PA over Peppol, and forwards the F1 copy to the PPF as the fifth corner.

The **PPF record document** step validates SIRENs and checks both parties against the Annuaire. Documents where either side is missing are rejected and should branch into the [reporting flow](/guides/fr-pa-reporting)  for non-regulated transactions.

### Invoice formats

The PA specification accepts five formats. In practice the three base formats below cover essentially all real-world traffic, and Invopop supports both sending and receiving on all three.

| Format | Notes |
|---|---|
| [UBL](/apps/oasis-ubl) | Peppol France CIUS — recommended default. Native Peppol format with the widest receiver support. |
| [CII](/apps/uncefact-cii) | UN/CEFACT XML, semantically equivalent to UBL. Common in cross-border and industrial contexts. |
| [Factur-X](/apps/uncefact-cii) | Hybrid PDF/A-3 with embedded CII. Identical to [ZUGFeRD](/guides/de-ubl) and aligned with EN 16931. |

Switching formats is a workflow configuration away rather than a data-model change. You author and validate one GOBL invoice, and the workflow's generation step produces whichever format the counterparty expects.

### Billing mode

Mandatory on every flux 2 and flux 10 B2B invoice and set in GOBL under `tax.ext.fr-ctc-billing-mode`. The prefix carries the nature of the operation: `B` goods (*biens*), `S` services, `M` mixed, plus the suffix the payment context.

| Goods/Services/Mixed | Payment context |
|---|---|
| `B1` `S1` `M1` | Standard submission (*dépôt d'une facture*) |
| `B2` `S2` `M2` | Already-paid invoice | 
| `B3` `S3` `M3` | Final invoice after down payment | 
| `B4` `S4` `M4` | Subcontractor invoice (payment delegation) | 
| `B5` `S5` `M5` | Co-contractor invoice | 
| `B6` `S6` `M6` | VAT already collected via e-reporting |


Suffix `7` covers invoices issued after the sale was already cashed in and reported through [e-reporting](/guides/fr-pa-reporting). For example, a company requesting a B2B invoice after paying a restaurant bill. Marking it `S7`/`B7` tells the PPF not to count the VAT a second time.

Suffix `1` is the ordinary case — a plain invoice submitted for payment. Left unset, the add-on defaults the mode to `M1` (`M2` when fully paid) — pin the prefix that matches the nature of the operation, as the [example invoices](/guides/fr-pa-invoicing#example-invoices) do.

### VAT tax keys → UNTDID 5305

GOBL sets the `untdid-tax-category` (and `cef-vatex` exemption reason where one applies) from the tax key on each line — you pick the key, the add-on derives the codes:

| GOBL tax key | Category | Exemption reason | Use for |
|---|---|---|---|
| `standard` | `S` | — | Domestic VAT at 20% / 10% / 5.5% / 2.1% |
| `zero` | `Z` | — | Zero-rated supplies |
| `exempt` | `E` | per note | Exempt operations (Art. 261 CGI — cite the article in a note) |
| `reverse-charge` | `AE` | `VATEX-EU-AE` | Autoliquidation, e.g. building subcontracting |
| `intra-community` | `K` | `VATEX-EU-IC` | Intra-EU B2B supplies |
| `export` | `G` | `VATEX-EU-G` | Exports outside the EU |
| `outside-scope` | `O` | `VATEX-EU-O` | Operations outside the scope of VAT |

The full field-level rules (permitted document types, VAT percents, and party identifier schemes) are maintained in the add-on.
