Romania uses **RO e-Factura**, the national e-invoicing system run by ANAF. Every invoice is converted to UBL following CIUS-RO, the Romanian profile of EN 16931, and uploaded to ANAF, which validates it and seals it with the Ministry of Finance signature. The sealed XML is the legal invoice: Invopop attaches it to the invoice once ANAF accepts it.

This guide assumes you have already connected the Chargebee app and completed the general setup — see the [Chargebee overview](/guides/chargebee) first if you haven't.

**Required apps:** [Romania](/apps/romania), [Forex](/guides/update-exchange-rates)

<Info>
  Before processing any invoices, the supplier must be registered and authorized with ANAF through Invopop. See the [RO e-Factura supplier guide](/guides/ro-efactura-supplier) for the registration steps, and the [RO e-Factura guide](/guides/ro-efactura) for how invoices are sent.
</Info>

## Workflow

The workflow imports the invoice from Chargebee, converts it to CIUS-RO XML, signs it and sends it to ANAF, then renders a PDF and updates Chargebee. The invoice keeps the number Chargebee gave it. Two steps prepare the invoice before it is converted:

- **Modify silo entry** adds the `ro-efactura-v1` addon, which applies the CIUS-RO rules on top of EN 16931.
- **Update exchange rates** sets the ECB rate to RON for the invoice date. ANAF requires the VAT of an invoice in another currency, such as EUR, to be stated in RON (`BR-RO-030`). Invoices in RON are left unchanged.

Keep **Sign envelope** before **Send e-Factura XML to ANAF**: Invopop stamps the invoice with the ANAF upload ID and response date after sending it, and only a signed invoice can be stamped.

## Chargebee setup

ANAF validates every invoice against the CIUS-RO rules, so a few details have to be in place in Chargebee before the first invoice is created. Chargebee copies them into each invoice when it is created: changing them later does not update invoices that already exist.

**1. Organization address**

The supplier's name and address on the invoice come from your Chargebee organization address, under **Settings → Configure Chargebee → Business Profile**. Fill in the company name, street, city, post code and country, and the county in the state field.

**2. Taxes**

Enable taxes under **Settings → Configure Chargebee → Taxes** and add Romania as a tax region with its VAT rates. Make sure the customers and charges you invoice are not marked as tax-exempt: a line without VAT is rejected by ANAF (`BR-CO-18`, `BR-CO-04`).

**3. Customer billing address**

Each customer needs a billing address with street, city and country. For customers in Romania, the county goes in the state field. For business customers, set their CIF in the VAT number field; customers without one are sent as consumers.

<Note>
  Write counties by their Romanian name or code: `Cluj`, `CJ` or `RO-CJ`. For Bucharest, use `București` (or `B`) as the state and the sector as the city, for example `Sector 1`. The English name `Bucharest` is not recognized.
</Note>
