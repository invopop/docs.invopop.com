## Introduction

An NFS-e (_Nota Fiscal de Serviços Eletrônica_) is the electronic invoice for services. The issuer's municipality authorizes it, with its own rules. You can find [the full list of supported municipalities here](https://tecnospeed.com.br/plugdfe/nfse/#homologacao).

The workflows, environments and cancel arguments are in the [DF-e overview](/guides/br-dfe). For onboarding suppliers, see the companion guide: [DF-e supplier registration](/guides/br-dfe-supplier).

## Prerequisites

To issue an NFS-e, you will need:

- A registered supplier: follow the [DF-e supplier registration guide](/guides/br-dfe-supplier) to connect the Documentos Fiscais Eletrônicos Brazil app and register the supplier's certificate before issuing.
- The "DF-e issue service invoice (NFS-e)" workflow, and the "DF-e cancel service invoice (NFS-e)" workflow to cancel. Both are in the [DF-e overview setup](/guides/br-dfe#setup).
- Customer details.
- Details of the services provided:
  - quantity,
  - price,
  - applicable tax rates (ISS, PIS, COFINS, etc.), and,
  - service code (_Código Item Lista Serviço_) as defined by the municipality.
- To have chosen an invoice series

## Cancel an invoice

Run the "DF-e cancel service invoice (NFS-e)" workflow over the silo entry you want to cancel. On success, the entry will be set to `Void`. The optional job arguments are in [Cancel arguments](/guides/br-dfe#cancel-arguments).

<Info>
  NFS-e cancellations are subject to the rules of each municipality. Some require a specific cancellation code.
</Info>
