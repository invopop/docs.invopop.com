## Introduction

The [DIAN](https://www.dian.gov.co/) is the Colombian government agency responsible for managing and overseeing tax collection, customs operations, and facilitating international trade.

Before issuing electronic invoices, a business must be registered with the DIAN with an active RUT (_Registro Único Tributario_), pass the DIAN enablement (_habilitación_) with its chosen technology provider, and hold an authorized numbering range for its invoices.

Invopop has partnered with [Alegra](https://www.alegra.com), a DIAN-authorized technology provider, to onboard suppliers and issue documents on their behalf. Registering a supplier involves three steps:

1. **Invopop:** a workflow creates the supplier's Alegra account and publishes a portal link.
2. **DIAN:** the supplier selects Alegra as their technology provider and submits the enablement test set through that portal.
3. **Numbering (live only):** the supplier links their authorized numbering range to Alegra in the DIAN portal.

Once a supplier is registered, continue with the companion guide: [Invoicing in Colombia](/guides/co-dian-invoicing).

<Warning>
  The previous Plemsi integration is deprecated and no longer maintained. If your suppliers still issue through Plemsi, follow [Migrate from Plemsi](#migrate-from-plemsi) to move them to this integration.
</Warning>

| - | Sandbox | Live |
|---|---|---|
| **Supplier** | Only Alegra's test NIT (`9005590882`) | Any Colombian supplier with an active RUT |
| **Enablement** | Simulated with fixed TestSetIds | DIAN enablement, once per supplier |
| **Resolution** | Fixed test resolution (`SETP`), seeded automatically | The supplier's numbering ranges linked to Alegra |

## Prerequisites

To register a supplier in Colombia, you will need:

- **Supplier details** to build the [GOBL](https://docs.gobl.org) party: legal name, NIT with the verification digit, and email. The address and the [`co-dian-municipality`](https://docs.gobl.org/addons/co-dian-v2) extension are optional here but required on the supplier's invoices (see [When is the municipality code required?](/guides/co-dian-invoicing#when-is-the-municipality-code-required)).
- **The supplier's legal representative**, with access to the email on their own RUT (the DIAN's [Habilitación portal](https://catalogo-vpfe-hab.dian.gov.co/User/Login) sends its login links there) and, for numbering, a MUISCA account with a DIAN electronic signature.
- For live issuing, a **numbering range authorization** (_resolución de numeración_) from the DIAN for each invoice series. See [Numbering resolutions](#numbering-resolutions).

### How it works

<Steps>
  <Step title="Set state → Processing">
    Sets the state of the silo entry to <Badge color="yellow">Processing</Badge>.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Register supplier">
    Validates the party's name, NIT, and email, creates the supplier's company in Alegra, and publishes a public enablement portal link under the `registration-link` key in the entry's meta section.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Wait for DIAN enablement">
    Pauses until the DIAN accepts the enablement test set the supplier submits from the portal. The step resumes automatically as soon as the DIAN responds. If the DIAN rejects the test set, the step fails and the workflow continues in its [error handling flow](/guides/workflows#error-handling); see [Failed enablement](#failed-enablement).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Set state → Registered">
    Sets the state of the silo entry to <Badge color="purple">Registered</Badge>.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Tip>
  To be notified of the outcome instead of polling, add a [Send Webhook](/guides/webhooks) step after **Wait for DIAN enablement** for the accepted case, and another in the error handling flow for failures. The failure payload carries the job's `faults`, including the DIAN's rejection reason.
</Tip>

## Register a supplier

Each supplier NIT can be registered once per workspace and environment. For each supplier:

<Steps>
  <Step title="Upload the supplier party">
    Create a silo entry with the supplier's details as a GOBL [`org.Party`](https://docs.gobl.org/draft-0/org/party) document, either from the Console or via the [API](/api-ref/silo/entries/create-an-entry-put):

    <Supplier />
  </Step>
  <Step title="Run the registration workflow">
    Run the workflow created during setup on the supplier entry, from the Console or by [creating a job](/api-ref/transform/jobs/create-a-job-post). The job creates the Alegra company and pauses at the **Wait for DIAN enablement** step.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Share the portal link">
    Open the supplier entry in the Console and copy the **registration-link** from its meta section. Share it with the supplier's legal representative: it is public and needs no Invopop account.

    Via the API, [fetch the silo entry](/api-ref/silo/entries/fetch-an-entry) and read the `link_url` of the `meta` row with the key `registration-link`:

    ```json
    {
      "meta": [
        {
          "key": "registration-link",
          "link_scope": "public",
          "link_url": "https://gov-co.invopop.app/portal/{silo_entry_id}"
        }
      ]
    }
    ```

    To skip the portal entirely, submit the TestSetId yourself as described in [Complete the enablement via the API](#complete-the-enablement-via-the-api).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Register as electronic invoicer at the DIAN">
    The legal representative opens the DIAN's [Habilitación portal](https://catalogo-vpfe-hab.dian.gov.co/User/Login) (also linked from [dian.gov.co](https://www.dian.gov.co/) under **Factura Electrónica**) and logs in as **Empresa** (or **Persona** for individuals). The DIAN emails an access link, valid for 60 minutes, to the address on the RUT of the person logging in, the legal representative for companies.

    <Frame caption="Habilitación login (DIAN)">
      <img width="600" src="/assets/guides/co-dian-login.png" alt="DIAN Habilitación login with the Empresa and Persona options highlighted" />
    </Frame>

    The first time they open **Registro y habilitación** → **Documentos electrónicos**, the **Registrar facturador** screen appears: they enter the **Correo electrónico para recepción de facturas** and click **Registrar**. They then log out and log back in with a new access link before continuing.

    <Frame caption="Registrar facturador (DIAN)">
      <img width="600" src="/assets/guides/co-dian-register-invoicer.png" alt="DIAN Registrar facturador screen with the Registrar button highlighted" />
    </Frame>
  </Step>
  <Step title="Select Alegra as technology provider at the DIAN">
    Back in the **Habilitación** portal, the legal representative:

    1. Goes to **Registro y habilitación** → **Documentos electrónicos** → **Factura electrónica** and clicks **Configurar modos de operación**.

    <Frame caption="Facturador electrónico (DIAN)">
      <img width="600" src="/assets/guides/co-dian-configure-operation-modes.png" alt="DIAN Facturador electrónico page with the Configurar modos de operación button highlighted" />
    </Frame>

    2. Under **Seleccione el modo de operación**, picks **Software de un proveedor tecnológico**.
    3. Under **Datos de empresa y software**, sets **Nombre empresa proveedora** to **Soluciones Alegra S.A.S** and **Nombre del software** to Alegra's software from the list, then clicks **Asociar**.

    <Frame caption="Configurar modos de operación, filled in for Alegra (DIAN)">
      <img width="600" src="/assets/guides/co-dian-operation-mode.png" alt="DIAN Configurar modos de operación form with the operation mode, provider and software fields and the Asociar button" />
    </Frame>

    4. Under **Listado de modos de operación asociados**, clicks the eye icon in **Acciones**. If asked for a **Tipo de documento**, they choose **Factura electrónica** and click **Set de pruebas**. They copy the **TestSetId** shown in the **¡Importante!** banner.

    <Frame caption="Listado de modos de operación asociados, with Alegra (DIAN)">
      <img width="600" src="/assets/guides/co-dian-operation-modes-list.png" alt="DIAN list of associated operation modes with the Acciones icons" />
    </Frame>

    <Frame caption="Set de pruebas with the TestSetId, for Alegra (DIAN)">
      <img width="600" src="/assets/guides/co-dian-test-set-id.png" alt="DIAN Set de pruebas page with the TestSetId highlighted in the Importante banner" />
    </Frame>

    The DIAN explains these screens in sections 1 and 2 of [Registration and operation mode selection](https://www.dian.gov.co/impuestos/factura-electronica/Documents/Registro-y-Seleccion-del-Modo-de-Operacion-Factura-Electronica.pdf).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Submit the test set">
    The legal representative pastes the TestSetId into the Invopop portal and clicks **Enviar set de pruebas**. Alegra sends the required test documents to the DIAN automatically, signed with its own digital certificate, so the supplier doesn't need one.

    <Frame caption="Enablement portal">
      <img width="600" src="/assets/guides/co-dian-enablement-portal.png" alt="Enablement portal with the DIAN TestSetId field and the Enviar set de pruebas button" />
    </Frame>

    To submit the TestSetId without the hosted portal, use the [Submit test set](/api-ref/apps/gov-co/enablement-submit) endpoint.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Wait for the DIAN response">
    The portal refreshes while the DIAN processes the test set. Once accepted, the workflow resumes and the entry moves to the registered state. The DIAN then asks the supplier for their production start date, when their RUT is updated with responsibility 52 (_Facturador electrónico_). If the DIAN rejects the test set, the workflow fails and the DIAN's reason is recorded in the job's faults; see [Failed enablement](#failed-enablement) for where to read it and how to start again.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Info>
  Re-running the registration workflow on the same entry is safe: it restarts a failed enablement, keeps a test set that is still waiting for the DIAN, and does nothing once the supplier is enabled.
</Info>

## Numbering resolutions

In live, every invoice series needs a DIAN numbering range authorization linked to Alegra. This isn't needed in the sandbox, which uses the fixed `SETP` test resolution. Each authorization is valid for 2 years.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Request the numbering range">
    In the DIAN's [MUISCA system](https://muisca.dian.gov.co/WebArquitectura/DefLogin.faces) (**Usuario Registrado** on dian.gov.co), under **Numeración de Facturación** → **Solicitar numeración de facturación**, the legal representative:

    <Frame caption="MUISCA menu (DIAN)">
      <img width="400" src="/assets/guides/co-dian-muisca-menu.png" alt="DIAN MUISCA menu with Numeración de Facturación and Solicitar Numeración de Facturación" />
    </Frame>

    1. Checks that the RUT is up to date (name, address, invoicing responsibility, and establishments).
    2. Clicks **Autorizar Rangos** and fills in **Prefijo** (up to four letters or digits, which becomes the invoice `series`), **Tipo Facturación** set to **FACTURA ELECTRÓNICA DE VENTA**, and **Rango Desde** / **Rango Hasta**, then clicks **Agregar**.
    3. Signs the request with their DIAN electronic signature (_firma electrónica_), first as form 1302 and then as form 1876, which the DIAN issues with the authorized ranges and validity.

    <Frame caption="Solicitud de autorización de rangos (DIAN)">
      <img width="600" src="/assets/guides/co-dian-numbering-request.png" alt="DIAN MUISCA Solicitud de Autorización de Rangos form with Prefijo, Tipo Facturación, Rango Desde and Rango Hasta fields" />
    </Frame>

    See [Request for invoice numbering authorization](https://www.dian.gov.co/impuestos/factura-electronica/Documents/Numeracion2022-AutorizacionF.pdf).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Link the range to Alegra">
    Once the DIAN has accepted the test set, and about two hours after the range request, log in to the production environment through [Facturando Electrónicamente](https://catalogo-vpfe.dian.gov.co/User/Login). Go to **Configuración** → **Rangos de numeración**, pick **Soluciones Alegra S.A.S** under **Proveedor - Software** and the range under **Prefijo**, and click **Agregar**.

    <Frame caption="Gestionar asociación de prefijos, with Alegra selected (DIAN)">
      <img width="600" src="/assets/guides/co-dian-numbering-prefix.png" alt="DIAN Gestionar Asociación de Prefijos page with the Proveedor - Software and Prefijo fields and the Agregar button" />
    </Frame>

    If production shows **Empresa no se encuentra habilitada**, or Alegra's operation mode doesn't appear there, click **Sincronizar Contribuyente a producción** on the **Facturador electrónico** page of the Habilitación portal:

    <Frame caption="Sincronizar contribuyente a producción (DIAN)">
      <img width="600" src="/assets/guides/co-dian-sync-production.png" alt="DIAN Facturador electrónico page with the Sincronizar Contribuyente a producción button highlighted" />
    </Frame>

    The DIAN explains this in sections 3 and 4 of [Registration and operation mode selection](https://www.dian.gov.co/impuestos/factura-electronica/Documents/Registro-y-Seleccion-del-Modo-de-Operacion-Factura-Electronica.pdf).
  </Step>
</Steps>

### Renew a range

A range with numbers left can be extended once it's within 15 business days of its expiry date; the new validity is set by the DIAN on the resulting form 1876. Once a range is used up or expired, request a new one as described above instead.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the range">
    In MUISCA, under **Numeración de Facturación** → **Solicitar numeración de facturación**, the legal representative clicks **Habilitar Rangos**.

    <Frame caption="Consultar numeración de facturación (DIAN)">
      <img width="600" src="/assets/guides/co-dian-numbering-renew.png" alt="DIAN MUISCA Consultar Numeración de Facturación screen with the Habilitar Rangos button highlighted" />
    </Frame>
  </Step>
  <Step title="Request the extension">
    They enter the same **Prefijo** and **Tipo Facturación**, click **Consultar**, review the range shown (editing **Rango Desde** if prompted), and click **Borrador**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Sign it">
    They sign form 1302 and then form 1876, which carries the new validity.
  </Step>
</Steps>

See [Request for invoice numbering extension](https://www.dian.gov.co/impuestos/factura-electronica/Documents/Numeracion2022-Habilitacion-F.pdf).

Invopop picks up new and renewed resolutions automatically on the next send. Check them anytime with [List resolutions](/api-ref/apps/gov-co/resolutions-list).

### Contingency range

To keep invoicing on paper when electronic invoicing isn't possible (see [Transmitting invoices issued during a contingency](/guides/co-dian-invoicing#transmitting-invoices-issued-during-a-contingency)), the supplier needs a separate range for paper invoices. Request it ahead of time following the same steps as above, with **Tipo Facturación** set to the paper invoice option (_factura de talonario o de papel_), then link it the same way. The DIAN doesn't assign a technical key to these ranges.

<Warning>
  Contingency resolutions can't be fetched from the DIAN, so once form 1876 is issued, upload the resolution to Invopop using [Upload contingency resolution](/api-ref/apps/gov-co/resolutions-upload). Without it, contingency invoices can't be sent.
</Warning>

See the DIAN's [contingency numbering and transcription guide](https://www.dian.gov.co/impuestos/factura-electronica/Documents/Paso-a-paso-solicitud-de-numeracion-y-transcripcion-de-la-factura-de-contingencia.pdf).

## Complete the enablement via the API

For platforms that want to drive the enablement programmatically instead of sharing the hosted portal, use the enablement API (both endpoints under `/apps/gov-co/v1/entries/{silo_entry_id}/enablement`). The entry must belong to the authenticated workspace and have been registered first.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Submit the test set">
    [Submit test set](/api-ref/apps/gov-co/enablement-submit) with the TestSetId from the DIAN enablement portal. It responds `400` when a test set is already awaiting the DIAN outcome, the supplier is already enabled, or a previous enablement failed. Run the registration workflow again to restart a failed enablement.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Track the outcome">
    [Fetch the enablement status](/api-ref/apps/gov-co/enablement-get): `status` moves from `pending` to `accepted` or `failed` once the DIAN responds. The workflow's **Wait for DIAN enablement** step picks up the outcome exactly as it does for portal submissions.

    The endpoint returns the status only. When it's `failed`, read the reason in the registration job's faults, as described in [Failed enablement](#failed-enablement).
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Failed enablement

When the DIAN rejects the test set, or the submission fails, the **Wait for DIAN enablement** step fails, the enablement status becomes `failed`, and the registration job continues in the workflow's [error handling flow](/guides/workflows#error-handling). The DIAN's reason isn't returned by the enablement endpoint; it's recorded in the [faults](/api-ref/transform/jobs/fetch-a-job#response-faults) of the registration job, under the `gov-co.wait.enablement` provider. Read it from any of:

- **The Console:** open the supplier entry and check its [job history](/console/workflow-history).
- **The API:** [fetch the job](/api-ref/transform/jobs/fetch-a-job) and read its `faults` array. Prefer the job's faults over the `faults` field on the silo entry.
- **A webhook:** with a [Send Webhook](/guides/webhooks) step in the error handling flow, the payload includes the same `faults`.

The fault message carries the reasons given by the DIAN or the technology provider, when they return any. Reasons that quote a DIAN validation rule (such as `Regla: FAJ40`) are explained in section 8 of the DIAN's [technical annex](https://www.dian.gov.co/impuestos/factura-electronica/Documents/Anexo-Tecnico-Factura-Electronica-de-Venta-vr-1-9.pdf), and section 7.12 lists the test set status codes.

To start again:

1. Fix the cause, for example by correcting the supplier details in the entry or getting a new TestSetId from the DIAN portal.
2. Re-run the registration workflow on the same entry. Until you do, [Submit test set](/api-ref/apps/gov-co/enablement-submit) responds `400`.
3. Submit the test set again, from the portal or via the API.

## Sandbox

The same registration flow runs in sandbox workspaces against the DIAN test environment, with a few differences:

- Only Alegra's own NIT (`9005590882`) can be registered, since the DIAN test registry accepts documents from that emitter alone. A NIT can be registered once per workspace, so each workspace has a single sandbox supplier.
- The fixed DIAN test resolution (series `SETP`, codes `990000000`–`995000000`) is seeded automatically on registration. The range is shared with other users testing in the sandbox, so pick codes unlikely to collide.
- The enablement is simulated: instead of a real DIAN TestSetId, enter one of the following values in the portal to choose the outcome.

| TestSetId | Simulated result |
|---|---|
| `a70562e0-631e-4ceb-aa65-36887b57dc17` | Accepted (completes the registration) |
| `r70562e0-631e-4ceb-aa65-36887b57dc17` | Rejected |
| `p70562e0-631e-4ceb-aa65-36887b57dc17` | Processing (stays pending) |
| `f70562e0-631e-4ceb-aa65-36887b57dc17` | Failed |

Use the accepted value to complete a supplier registration end to end; the other values let you test how your integration handles rejected, pending, and failed outcomes. Submit the rejected or failed value to exercise your [failed enablement](#failed-enablement) handling end to end, from reading the job's faults to re-running the workflow.

## Migrate from Plemsi

Moving a supplier from the old Plemsi integration takes the same steps as a new registration. At the DIAN, the supplier simply adds Alegra as another technology provider.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Set up the Colombia app">
    Connect the app and create the registration workflow as described in [Setup](#setup), and the send workflow from the [invoicing guide](/guides/co-dian-invoicing#setup).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Register the supplier">
    Follow [Register a supplier](#register-a-supplier): run the registration workflow, add Alegra as technology provider at the DIAN, and submit the test set. The Plemsi enablement doesn't carry over, so this is needed even for suppliers already issuing.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Set up the numbering ranges">
    Under **Configuración** → **Rangos de numeración** (see [Numbering resolutions](#numbering-resolutions)), choose one of:

    - **Move the existing range:** unlink it from Plemsi (the ✕ icon next to it in the list) and link it to Alegra. The series stays the same, and new invoices must continue after the last number issued through Plemsi.
    - **Add a new range:** request a new range with a new prefix and link it to Alegra. Invoices sent through Alegra start a fresh series, while the old range stays with Plemsi until it's no longer needed.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Switch to the new send workflow">
    Send new invoices with the Colombia send workflow, using the series from the previous step. Once they clear, stop using the Plemsi workflow.
  </Step>
</Steps>

At this point, you're ready to start sending invoices on behalf of the supplier. Head over to the [Invoicing in Colombia guide](/guides/co-dian-invoicing) to continue.
