The document specs tell you how to build each GOBL document for Mexico. They are a reference for the engineer who writes the integration.

## Which document do you need?

| Your case | Document | GOBL schema |
|---|---|---|
| You sell to a customer who needs an invoice | [Invoice](/guides/mx-cfdi-invoice) | `bill/invoice`, type `standard` |
| You sell to a consumer who gives no RFC | [Invoice](/guides/mx-cfdi-invoice) without a `customer` | `bill/invoice`, type `standard` |
| You add together the consumer sales of a period | [Global invoice](/guides/mx-cfdi-global) | `bill/invoice`, tag `global` |
| You decrease the amount of an invoice | [Credit note](/guides/mx-cfdi-credit-note) | `bill/invoice`, type `credit-note` |
| A customer pays an invoice that was not paid at issue | [Payment receipt](/guides/mx-cfdi-payment) | `bill/payment`, type `receipt` |
| You remove an invoice that must not exist | [Cancellation guide](/guides/mx-sat-cancelling) | `bill/status` |

All the documents use the same [party rules](/guides/mx-cfdi-parties) for the supplier and the customer.

## How the specs are made

- **One row for each government field.** Each spec lists every field of the SAT schema for its node, also the fields that Invopop does not write.
- **Checked against the SAT schema.** A script compares the rows with `cfdv40.xsd` and `Pagos20.xsd`.
- **Checked against the code.** The same script compares the rule numbers with the GOBL addon `mx-cfdi-v4`.
- **Real output.** The "CFDI output" in each example comes from the Invopop converter [`gobl.mx.cfdi`](https://github.com/invopop/gobl.mx.cfdi) v0.69.0. It is the XML before the seal and the PAC stamp.
- **Standard behavior only.** The specs do not include rules that are specific to one customer.
