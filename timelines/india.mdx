## Timeline

Current and upcoming regulation for India.

<Update label="1 January 2027" tags={["B2B", "Reverse charge", "E-way bill"]}>
  **E-invoices for reverse-charge self-invoices**<br />
  The buyer must get an IRN for its reverse-charge self-invoices (Notification 04/2026-Central Tax). This covers purchases from unregistered suppliers (`RCMDOM`) and imports of services (`RCMIMP`). The INV-01 schema moves to version 1.2 (Notification 05/2026-Central Tax). The law applies from 1 January 2027, but GSTN has not yet announced the production date on the IRPs. The e-way bill number is planned to grow from 12 to 14 digits on the same date.
</Update>

<Update label="Now" tags={["B2B", "B2G", "Exports"]}>
  **E-invoicing above Rs 5 crore turnover**<br />
  Registered persons whose aggregate turnover exceeds Rs 5 crore in any preceding financial year from 2017-18 must e-invoice. They report B2B, B2G and export documents to an IRP. Suppliers with turnover of Rs 10 crore or more must report each document within 30 days of its date. Six IRPs are live, and IRN generation is free on all of them.

  | System | Description | Invopop coverage |
  |---|---|---|
  | [IRP e-invoicing](/compliance/india) | Clearance of B2B, B2G and export invoices through the six IRPs | Not available |
  | [B2C dynamic QR code](/compliance/india) | Payment QR code on B2C invoices above Rs 500 crore turnover | Not available |

</Update>

<Update label="1 April 2025" tags={["B2B"]}>
  **30-day reporting limit from Rs 10 crore**<br />
  Suppliers with turnover of Rs 10 crore or more can no longer report documents older than 30 days. Multi-factor authentication becomes mandatory for all users of the e-invoice and e-way bill portals.
</Update>

<Update label="1 November 2023" tags={["B2B"]}>
  **30-day reporting limit for the largest suppliers**<br />
  Suppliers with aggregate annual turnover above Rs 100 crore can no longer report documents older than 30 days.
</Update>

<Update label="1 August 2023" tags={["B2B", "B2G", "Exports"]}>
  **Threshold of Rs 5 crore**<br />
  E-invoicing applies to registered persons whose aggregate turnover exceeds Rs 5 crore (Notification 10/2023-Central Tax).
</Update>

<Update label="March 2023" tags={["B2B"]}>
  **Four private IRPs**<br />
  GSTN launches e-invoice registration through four private IRPs (Cygnet, Clear, EY and IRIS), next to the two NIC portals.
</Update>

<Update label="2020 – 2022" tags={["B2B", "B2G", "Exports", "B2C"]}>
  **How e-invoicing became mandatory**<br />
  Rule 48(4) of the CGST Rules created the obligation in December 2019. The turnover threshold then came down in steps:

  - **1 October 2020**: above Rs 500 crore. Supplies to registered persons and exports.
  - **1 December 2020**: dynamic QR code on B2C invoices for suppliers above Rs 500 crore.
  - **1 January 2021**: above Rs 100 crore.
  - **1 April 2021**: above Rs 50 crore.
  - **1 June 2021**: government departments and local authorities excluded.
  - **1 April 2022**: above Rs 20 crore.
  - **1 October 2022**: above Rs 10 crore.
</Update>
