## Timeline

Current and upcoming regulation for Malaysia.

<Update label="Now" tags={["B2B", "B2G", "B2C"]}>
  **MyInvois in force for all phases**<br />
  All four phases of the MyInvois mandate are in force. Taxpayers with annual turnover below RM3 million are exempt. Suppliers submit each e-invoice to MyInvois for validation and then share it with the buyer. Phase 4 businesses have a relaxation period until 31 December 2027.

  | System | Description | Invopop coverage |
  |---|---|---|
  | [MyInvois](/compliance/malaysia) | Real-time clearance of B2B, B2G and B2C e-invoices by LHDN | Coming soon |
  | [Peppol PINT-MY](/compliance/malaysia) | Optional delivery channel through MDEC-accredited providers | Not available |

</Update>

<Update label="1 August 2024" tags={["B2B", "B2G", "B2C"]}>
  **Phase 1**<br />
  MyInvois becomes mandatory for taxpayers with annual turnover above RM100 million.
</Update>

<Update label="1 January 2025" tags={["B2B", "B2G", "B2C"]}>
  **Phase 2**<br />
  MyInvois becomes mandatory for taxpayers with annual turnover above RM25 million, up to RM100 million.
</Update>

<Update label="1 July 2025" tags={["B2B", "B2G", "B2C"]}>
  **Phase 3 and wider SST scope**<br />
  MyInvois becomes mandatory for taxpayers with annual turnover above RM5 million, up to RM25 million. On the same day, the scope of Sales Tax and Service Tax becomes wider.
</Update>

<Update label="1 January 2026" tags={["B2B", "B2G", "B2C"]}>
  **Phase 4 and new consolidation limits**<br />
  MyInvois becomes mandatory for taxpayers with annual turnover up to RM5 million, above the RM3 million exemption floor. Consolidated e-invoices are no longer allowed for a single transaction above RM10,000, in all industries.
</Update>

<Update label="1 July 2026" tags={["B2B", "B2G", "B2C"]}>
  **New businesses**<br />
  Businesses that started between 2023 and 2025 with turnover of at least RM3 million, and all businesses that start from 2026, must comply.
</Update>

<Update label="1 August 2026" tags={["B2B", "B2G", "B2C"]}>
  **TIN and BRN validation**<br />
  MyInvois starts to validate the tax identification number (TIN) and the business registration number (BRN) of the parties.
</Update>

<Update label="31 December 2027" tags={["B2B", "B2G", "B2C"]}>
  **End of the Phase 4 relaxation period**<br />
  The relaxation period for Phase 4 businesses ends. After this date, they must follow all e-invoice rules, including the limits on consolidated e-invoices.
</Update>
