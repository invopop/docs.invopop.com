## Timeline

Current and upcoming regulation for Croatia.

<Update label="Now" tags={["B2B", "B2G", "B2C"]}>
  **Systems in force today**<br />
  Croatia's **Fiscalization 2.0** (eRačun) is live since 1 January 2026. VAT-registered businesses established in Croatia exchange structured e-invoices over the national 4-corner network and report each invoice to the tax authority. Public bodies receive e-invoices through FINA's central platform. Croatian suppliers reach it over the national network, and foreign suppliers reach it over Peppol. Consumer sales stay on the older receipt system.

  | System | Description | Invopop coverage |
  |---|---|---|
  | [eRačun (Fiscalization 2.0)](/compliance/croatia) | B2B and B2G e-invoicing over the national 4-corner network, with reporting to the tax authority | Work in progress |
  | [B2G over Peppol](/compliance/croatia#invoicing-public-bodies-b2g) | Invoices from foreign suppliers to Croatian public bodies, in Peppol BIS Billing 3.0 | Yes, with the [Peppol app](/apps/peppol) |
  | [Receipt fiscalization (B2C)](/compliance/croatia) | Real-time reporting of consumer receipts | Work in progress |

</Update>

<Update label="1 July 2019" tags={["B2G"]}>
  **B2G e-invoicing mandatory**<br />
  Under the Act on e-invoicing in public procurement (NN 94/18), all suppliers must send structured e-invoices to public bodies. FINA runs the central platform that receives them, with a Peppol access point for foreign suppliers.
</Update>

<Update label="1 September 2025" tags={["B2B", "B2G"]}>
  **New Fiscalization Act in force**<br />
  Croatia's Fiscalization Act (NN 89/25) comes into force. It sets the rules for structured e-invoicing between businesses and for the reporting of each invoice to the tax authority.
</Update>

<Update label="1 January 2026" tags={["B2B", "B2G", "E-reporting"]}>
  **Mandatory for VAT-registered businesses**<br />
  VAT-registered Croatian businesses must issue and receive domestic e-invoices and report each one to the tax authority. This reporting also applies to their invoices to public bodies. Businesses that are not VAT-registered must receive e-invoices. Consumer receipt fiscalization now applies to all payment methods.
</Update>

<Update label="1 January 2027" tags={["B2B", "B2G"]}>
  **Extended to remaining businesses**<br />
  Businesses and public bodies that are not VAT-registered must also issue e-invoices. They can use the free MIKROeRAČUN app from the tax authority.
</Update>

<Update label="~1 July 2030" tags={["E-reporting"]}>
  **EU-wide digital reporting (ViDA)**<br />
  The EU's VAT in the Digital Age initiative is expected to introduce harmonized digital reporting across member states.
</Update>
