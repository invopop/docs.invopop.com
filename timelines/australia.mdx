## Timeline

Current and upcoming regulation for Australia.

<Update label="Now" tags={["B2G", "B2B"]}>
  **E-invoicing systems enforced to date**<br />
  Australia uses the 4-corner Peppol network, with the ATO as Australian Peppol Authority. Federal government entities (NCEs) must be able to receive e-invoices since 1 July 2022, and they are moving to eInvoicing as the default. PINT A-NZ is the only invoice specification on the network. B2B e-invoicing is voluntary, and B2C is out of scope.

  | System        | Description  | Invopop Coverage |
  |---------------|---------------|---------------|
  | [Peppol PINT A-NZ](/compliance/australia#invoicing-in-australia) | B2G and voluntary B2B e-invoicing through the Peppol network | Work in progress |
</Update>

<Update label="25 October 2018" tags={["B2G", "B2B"]}>
  **Trans-Tasman Electronic Invoicing Arrangement**<br />
  Australia and New Zealand agree on a common approach to e-invoicing. Both countries later adopt Peppol and one shared invoice specification.
</Update>

<Update label="31 October 2019" tags={["Infrastructure"]}>
  **The ATO becomes the Australian Peppol Authority**<br />
  The Australian Taxation Office takes on the Peppol Authority role. It sets the Australian requirements and accredits Peppol service providers.
</Update>

<Update label="1 January 2020" tags={["B2G"]}>
  **5-day payment for e-invoices**<br />
  Under the Supplier Pay On-Time or Pay Interest Policy (RMG 417), federal entities pay e-invoices within 5 days, for contracts up to $1 million.
</Update>

<Update label="1 July 2022" tags={["B2G"]}>
  **Federal entities must receive e-invoices**<br />
  Non-corporate Commonwealth entities (NCEs) must be able to receive e-invoices. The 5-day payment term now applies to all contract values.
</Update>

<Update label="15 May 2025" tags={["B2G", "B2B"]}>
  **PINT A-NZ becomes mandatory**<br />
  PINT A-NZ Billing becomes the only mandatory invoice specification. The earlier A-NZ Peppol BIS Billing 3.0 is removed from the network on 31 March 2026.
</Update>

<Update label="1 July 2026" tags={["B2G"]}>
  **eInvoicing default: 30% target**<br />
  NCEs aim to receive 30% of all their invoices through eInvoicing.
</Update>

<Update label="December 2026" tags={["B2G"]}>
  **eInvoicing default: automation**<br />
  NCEs enable automated processing and sending of e-invoices.
</Update>
