## Timeline

Current and upcoming regulation for Finland.

<Update label="Now" tags={["B2G", "B2B"]}>
  **E-invoicing systems enforced to date**<br />
  Under Act 241/2019, public contracting entities must receive e-invoices: central government since 1 April 2019, and all other contracting entities since 1 April 2020. Since 1 April 2021, the state receives only invoices that follow EN 16931. Since 1 April 2020, public buyers and businesses with a turnover above EUR 10,000 can ask each other for e-invoices. Most invoices travel on the domestic operator network as Finvoice or TEAPPSXML. Peppol is the second network, with the State Treasury (Valtiokonttori) as Peppol Authority.

  | System        | Description  | Invopop Coverage |
  |---------------|---------------|---------------|
  | [Domestic operator network](/compliance/finland#domestic-operator-network-finvoice-and-teappsxml) | B2B and B2G e-invoices between operators and banks, as Finvoice or TEAPPSXML | Yes |
  | [Peppol](/compliance/finland#peppol-network) | B2G and B2B e-invoices on Peppol BIS Billing 3.0 | Yes |

<Update label="1 July 2030" tags={["B2B", "E-reporting"]}>
  **ViDA digital reporting for intra-EU supplies**<br />
  The EU's VAT in the Digital Age (ViDA) directive brings digital reporting requirements for intra-EU supplies in all member states, Finland included.
</Update>

<Update label="2026" tags={["B2B"]}>
  **State Treasury consultation on a wider e-invoicing obligation**<br />
  From 2 February to 13 March 2026, the State Treasury consulted on options for e-invoices, from a general obligation to no change. In May 2026 it sent the results to the ministries. No bill exists yet.
</Update>

<Update label="1 January 2026" tags={["VAT"]}>
  **Reduced VAT rate falls to 13.5%**<br />
  The reduced VAT rate changes from 14% to 13.5%.
</Update>

<Update label="31 December 2024" tags={["Infrastructure"]}>
  **Only the 0216 Peppol scheme stays for Finland**<br />
  Peppol removes the older Finnish identifier schemes 0037, 0212, 0213 and 0215. Finnish organisations use their OVT code with the scheme 0216.
</Update>

<Update label="1 September 2024" tags={["VAT"]}>
  **General VAT rate rises to 25.5%**<br />
  The general VAT rate changes from 24% to 25.5%.
</Update>

<Update label="1 April 2024" tags={["B2G"]}>
  **Peppol ordering for central government**<br />
  Central government starts to use Peppol orders and order responses with its suppliers.
</Update>

<Update label="2022" tags={["B2G", "Infrastructure"]}>
  **Valtiokonttori becomes the national Peppol Authority**<br />
  The Finnish State Treasury joins OpenPeppol in July 2022 and sets up its Peppol Authority function in autumn 2022. It supervises Peppol service providers that serve Finnish organisations.
</Update>

<Update label="6 April 2021" tags={["B2G", "B2B"]}>
  **Operators validate against EN 16931**<br />
  E-invoice operators start to validate invoices against the European standard. They reject an invoice that fails, and the sender gets an error message.
</Update>

<Update label="1 April 2021" tags={["B2G"]}>
  **EN 16931 only for the state**<br />
  The state receives only invoices that follow EN 16931. The State Treasury moved this date from 2020 because of COVID-19.
</Update>

<Update label="1 April 2020" tags={["B2G", "B2B"]}>
  **All contracting entities receive e-invoices, and the right to request starts**<br />
  All Finnish contracting entities must receive e-invoices. Public buyers and businesses with a turnover above EUR 10,000 can ask each other for e-invoices.
</Update>

<Update label="1 April 2019" tags={["B2G"]}>
  **Act 241/2019 takes effect**<br />
  The Act on Electronic Invoicing by Contracting Entities and Traders takes effect. Central government must receive and process e-invoices.
</Update>
