## Timeline

Current and upcoming regulation for the UAE.

<Update label="Now" tags={["B2B", "B2G"]}>
  **Pilot and voluntary phase**<br />
  The UAE **Electronic Invoicing System** is open for voluntary use since 1 July 2026. Accredited Service Providers (ASPs) exchange PINT AE invoices over Peppol and report each one to the Federal Tax Authority. Mandatory use starts on 1 January 2027 for businesses with revenue of AED 50 million or more. These businesses must appoint an ASP by 30 October 2026.

  | System | Description | Invopop coverage |
  |---|---|---|
  | [Electronic Invoicing System](/compliance/uae) | B2B and B2G e-invoicing in PINT AE through ASPs, with reporting to the Federal Tax Authority | Planned |

</Update>

<Update label="March 2025" tags={["B2B", "B2G"]}>
  **Rules for service providers**<br />
  Ministerial Decision No. 64 of 2025 sets the rules to become an Accredited Service Provider (ASP). Only an ASP can exchange and report e-invoices in the UAE.
</Update>

<Update label="September 2025" tags={["B2B", "B2G"]}>
  **Scope and phases published**<br />
  The Ministry of Finance publishes Ministerial Decision No. 243 of 2025 on the Electronic Invoicing System and Ministerial Decision No. 244 of 2025 on its phases.
</Update>

<Update label="December 2025" tags={["B2B", "B2G"]}>
  **Penalties set**<br />
  Cabinet Decision No. 106 of 2025 sets the penalties. For example, a business with no ASP by its deadline pays AED 5,000 for each month of delay.
</Update>

<Update label="May 2026" tags={["B2B"]}>
  **ASP deadline extended**<br />
  Ministerial Decision No. 66 of 2026 moves the ASP deadline for businesses with revenue of AED 50 million or more from 31 July 2026 to 30 October 2026. The go-live date of 1 January 2027 does not change. The Ministry of Finance says that no further extensions will be granted.
</Update>

<Update label="1 July 2026" tags={["B2B", "B2G"]}>
  **Pilot and voluntary use start**<br />
  A pilot group of taxpayers that the Ministry of Finance invites starts to use the system. Any other business can also start voluntarily. Voluntary users follow all technical rules but get no e-invoicing penalties.
</Update>

<Update label="30 October 2026" tags={["B2B"]}>
  **ASP deadline for large businesses**<br />
  Businesses with revenue of AED 50 million or more must appoint an Accredited Service Provider.
</Update>

<Update label="1 January 2027" tags={["B2B", "B2G"]}>
  **Mandatory for large businesses**<br />
  Businesses with revenue of AED 50 million or more must issue, receive and report e-invoices. Transactions between members of the same VAT group get a 24-month grace period from this date.
</Update>

<Update label="31 March 2027" tags={["B2B", "B2G"]}>
  **ASP deadline for other businesses and government entities**<br />
  Businesses with revenue below AED 50 million and government entities must appoint an Accredited Service Provider.
</Update>

<Update label="1 July 2027" tags={["B2B", "B2G"]}>
  **Mandatory for other businesses**<br />
  Businesses with revenue below AED 50 million must issue, receive and report e-invoices.
</Update>

<Update label="1 October 2027" tags={["B2G"]}>
  **Mandatory for government entities**<br />
  Government entities must issue, receive and report e-invoices.
</Update>

<Update label="1 January 2029" tags={["B2B"]}>
  **End of the VAT group grace period**<br />
  E-invoicing applies in full to transactions between members of the same VAT group.
</Update>
