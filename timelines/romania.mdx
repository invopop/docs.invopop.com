## Timeline

Current and upcoming regulation for Romania.

<Update label="Now" tags={["B2B", "B2G", "B2C"]}>
  **RO e-Factura in force for all domestic invoices**<br />
  Taxable persons established in Romania send every domestic invoice to RO e-Factura within 5 working days of issue. The platform validates and seals B2B and B2G invoices and delivers them to the buyer in SPV. Suppliers report their invoices to consumers to the platform.

  | System | Description | Invopop coverage |
  |---|---|---|
  | [RO e-Factura](/compliance/romania) | National clearance platform for B2B and B2G invoices, and reporting of B2C invoices | Coming soon |

</Update>

<Update label="November 2021" tags={["B2B", "B2G"]}>
  **Platform available for voluntary use**<br />
  OUG 120/2021 creates RO e-Factura. Businesses can start to use it voluntarily.
</Update>

<Update label="1 July 2022" tags={["B2G"]}>
  **B2G e-invoicing mandatory**<br />
  Law 139/2022 makes RO e-Factura mandatory for invoices to public institutions.
</Update>

<Update label="1 January 2024" tags={["B2B"]}>
  **B2B reporting**<br />
  Under Law 296/2023, suppliers must report domestic B2B invoices to RO e-Factura.
</Update>

<Update label="1 July 2024" tags={["B2B"]}>
  **Full B2B clearance**<br />
  The sealed XML in RO e-Factura becomes the legal invoice for domestic B2B sales (Law 296/2023 and OUG 115/2023).
</Update>

<Update label="1 January 2025" tags={["B2C", "E-reporting"]}>
  **B2C reporting**<br />
  Suppliers must report invoices to consumers to RO e-Factura (OUG 69/2024 and OUG 138/2024). Simplified invoices come into scope, except qualifying fiscal cash register receipts.
</Update>

<Update label="1 July 2025" tags={["B2C"]}>
  **B2C penalties start**<br />
  After a six month grace period, penalties apply to B2C reporting.
</Update>

<Update label="1 August 2025" tags={["VAT"]}>
  **New VAT rates**<br />
  The standard VAT rate becomes 21% and the single reduced rate becomes 11%.
</Update>

<Update label="1 January 2026" tags={["B2B", "B2G", "B2C"]}>
  **5 working day deadline**<br />
  Under OUG 89/2025, the deadline to send an invoice becomes 5 working days from issue, instead of 5 calendar days. Invoices to buyers that are registered for VAT in Romania but not established there must also be reported.
</Update>

<Update label="1 June 2026" tags={["B2B"]}>
  **Voluntary use for individuals under CNP**<br />
  Under Law 88/2026, individuals who work under their personal ID number (CNP) and farmers under the special regime become optional users of RO e-Factura.
</Update>

<Update label="1 July 2030" tags={["Cross-border", "E-reporting"]}>
  **EU digital reporting (ViDA)**<br />
  The EU's VAT in the Digital Age initiative starts digital reporting for intra-EU transactions. Intra-Community supplies, which are out of scope today, come into scope at EU level.
</Update>
