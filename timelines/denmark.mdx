## Timeline

Current and upcoming regulation for Denmark.

<Update label="2005–2019" tags={["B2G", "Denmark"]}>
  **B2G e-invoicing mandate established and expanded**<br />
  Denmark became the first country in Europe to mandate electronic invoicing to public authorities.

  | System | Description | Invopop Coverage |
  |--------|-------------|-----------------|
  | [NemHandel / OIOUBL](/compliance/denmark) | Electronic invoicing to all Danish public authorities via NemHandel, using the OIOUBL format. Mandatory since February 2005. | Yes |
  | [Peppol BIS Billing 3.0](/compliance/denmark) | From April 2019, all public authorities must also accept invoices compliant with the EU standard EN 16931 via Peppol. | Yes |

<Update label="Now" tags={["B2B", "B2G", "Denmark"]}>
  **Digital Bookkeeping Act — phased rollout in progress**<br />
  Denmark's 2022 Bookkeeping Act requires businesses to use certified digital bookkeeping systems capable of issuing and receiving structured e-invoices (Peppol BIS 3.0 and OIOUBL) and generating SAF-T files on demand.

  | Phase | Scope | Date |
  |-------|-------|------|
  | Phase 1 | Medium/large companies (classes B, C, D) using certified standard systems | 1 Jul 2024 |
  | Phase 2 | Same companies using custom/self-developed systems | 1 Jan 2025 |
  | Phase 3 | All businesses with turnover > DKK 300,000 for two consecutive years, incl. foreign entities with Danish VAT registration | 1 Jan 2026 |
  | Phase 4 | Remaining businesses using in-house developed systems | No earlier than 1 Jul 2026 |

<Update label="18 May 2026" tags={["B2G", "Denmark"]}>
  **Updated OIOUBL validation rules mandatory**<br />
  Schematron validation rules [v1.17.2](https://nemhandel.dk/opdateringer-releases) for OIOUBL 2.1 became mandatory for all NemHandel e-invoicing, following the January 2026 cancellation of OIOUBL 3.0.
</Update>

<Update label="≈ 2028" tags={["B2B", "Denmark"]}>
  **Domestic e-reporting obligation (estimate)**<br />
  Denmark is expected to introduce a domestic digital reporting requirement for B2B transactions, potentially replacing the annual VAT client listing. The Bookkeeping Act already authorizes the government to establish a common public data reception for e-documents, laying the groundwork for a future continuous transaction control (CTC) model. Nothing has been formally announced — Denmark's stated focus is ViDA readiness by 2030.
</Update>

<Update label="2028 – mid-2029" tags={["B2B", "B2G", "Denmark"]}>
  **Migration to NemHandel BIS 4, the single unified format**<br />
  Following the cancellation of OIOUBL 3.0, Erhvervsstyrelsen announced [NemHandel BIS 4](https://www.vatupdate.com/2026/03/23/denmark-unveils-nemhandel-bis-4-e-invoicing-standard-cancels-oioubl-3-0-sets-2029-migration-timeline/) in February 2026 — a Danish implementation of Peppol BIS 4 using a PINT-like methodology — as the single standard replacing the dual-format landscape (OIOUBL 2.1 + Peppol BIS 3.0). Technical specifications are defined with stakeholder workgroups through 2027; release candidates and gradual migration run from 2028, and NemHandel BIS 4 becomes the primary standard by mid-2029. OIOUBL 2.1 remains in force until July 2029.
</Update>

<Update label="1 Jul 2030" tags={["B2B", "EU"]}>
  **VAT in the Digital Age (ViDA) — cross-border e-invoicing**<br />
  The EU ViDA Directive (adopted March 2025) requires mandatory e-invoicing and digital reporting for all intra-EU B2B transactions. Denmark, as an EU member state, must comply by this date. This is expected to accelerate the introduction of a domestic B2B e-invoicing transmission mandate ahead of the deadline.
</Update>
