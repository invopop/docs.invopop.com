---
title: "Fetch general terms"
description: "Fetch Invopop's general terms for a silo entry as Markdown, to show the signer before they accept them."
openapi: "GET /apps/gov-fr/v1/entry/{silo_entry_id}/terms"
---

Returns the general terms the signer accepts, as Markdown, so you can show them in your own interface. Once the signer has read them, accept them with `POST /sign`, or with `POST /agreement` when uploading a mandate signed outside Invopop, then call `POST /confirm`.
