---
title: "List a silo entry's migrations"
description: "List every takeover request for the address registered by a silo entry, newest first."
openapi: "GET /apps/gov-fr/v1/migrations/{silo_entry_id}"
---

Returns one item for every migration related to the endpoint registered by the silo entry, open and closed, refused ones included. The newest item shows where the address stands: an incoming item with `status` `pending` is waiting for your answer, and once answered its `decisions` hold the answer sent to the new PA. A silo entry has at most one open migration at a time.

Paginate with `offset` and `limit` (default `25`, maximum `200`). When more results remain, the response carries a `next_offset` to pass on the next call; it is omitted on the last page.
