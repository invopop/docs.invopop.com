---
title: "Fetch registration"
description: "Fetch a Croatian supplier's B2C fiscalization registration."
openapi: "GET /apps/gov-hr/v1/entry/{silo_entry_id}/certificate"
---

Returns the B2C fiscalization registration of the supplier in a party silo entry: its OIB, whether it is registered, and when its certificate expires. Use it to check a supplier before you send its invoices, and to remind it to upload a new certificate before the current one expires.

A registration belongs to the supplier's OIB in the workspace and the CIS environment, not to one silo entry. A sandbox workspace sees the registration for CIS test, and a live workspace the one for production CIS. Two party entries with the same OIB in the same workspace share one registration.

The answer of a supplier that is not registered has no dates:

```json
{
  "oib": "80432461483",
  "environment": "sandbox",
  "registered": false
}
```
