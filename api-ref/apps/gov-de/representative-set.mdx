---
title: "Set representative"
description: "Set the legal representative of the company being onboarded."
openapi: "POST /apps/gov-de/v1/entry/{silo_entry_id}/representative"
---

Sets the legal representative who completes the onboarding. Without this request, the onboarding uses the first person of the party.

The request also sends the current version of the party to the onboarding. A changed representative or party removes the documents uploaded so far.
