---
title: "Confirm onboarding"
description: "Submit the onboarding, which is decided at once."
openapi: "POST /apps/gov-de/v1/entry/{silo_entry_id}/confirm"
---

Submits the onboarding. It requires the accepted terms of service, the ID document and the proof of ownership.

The onboarding is decided at once. When it is approved, the **Wait for supplier approval** step continues the registration workflow. When it is rejected, the response carries the reason in `key` and `notes`.
