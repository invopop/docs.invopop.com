---
title: "Upload proof of ownership"
description: "Upload a document that shows that the legal representative can act for the company."
openapi: "POST /apps/gov-de/v1/entry/{silo_entry_id}/ownership"
---

Stores the proof of ownership, such as an extract from the commercial register (*Handelsregisterauszug*). Call before `POST /confirm`. Uploading again replaces the earlier document.
