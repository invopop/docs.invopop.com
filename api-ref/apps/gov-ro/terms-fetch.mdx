---
title: "Fetch terms and conditions"
openapi: "GET /apps/gov-ro/v1/entry/{silo_entry_id}/terms"
---

Returns the RO e-Factura terms and conditions as an HTML page, so you can show them to your customer in your own interface before accepting them on their behalf.

<Note>
  Run the **Register with ANAF** step on the party first. See the [white label registration](/guides/ro-efactura-supplier#white-label-registration) flow.
</Note>
