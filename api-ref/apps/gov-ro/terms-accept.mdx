---
title: "Confirm connection notice"
openapi: "POST /apps/gov-ro/v1/entry/{silo_entry_id}/terms"
---

Records that your customer confirmed the RO e-Factura connection notice and returns the ANAF authorization link. Send the link to the certificate holder, who completes the authorization on ANAF's site.

The confirmed notice is stored on the party entry as a private agreement file, together with its version, the confirmation time and the `api` channel. Calling the endpoint again returns the same answer.

<Note>
  Only call this endpoint once your customer has read the notice and authorized the connection. If the notice changes, the party has to confirm the new version before the link is handed out again.
</Note>
