---
title: "Accept terms"
openapi: "POST /apps/gov-ro/v1/entry/{silo_entry_id}/terms"
---

Records that your customer accepted the terms and returns the ANAF authorization link. Send the link to the certificate holder, who completes the authorization on ANAF's site.

Invopop records the acceptance together with the exact terms accepted. Calling the endpoint again returns the same answer.

<Note>
  Only call this endpoint once your customer has read and accepted the terms. The terms are accepted on every registration, so call it again each time the **Register with ANAF** step runs, including a renewal.
</Note>
