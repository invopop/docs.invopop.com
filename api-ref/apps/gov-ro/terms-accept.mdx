---
title: "Accept terms and conditions"
openapi: "POST /apps/gov-ro/v1/entry/{silo_entry_id}/terms"
---

Accepts the RO e-Factura terms and conditions for the party and returns the ANAF authorization link. Send the link to the certificate holder, who completes the authorization on ANAF's site.

The accepted terms are stored on the party entry as a private agreement file, together with their version, the acceptance time and the `api` channel. Calling the endpoint again returns the same answer.

<Note>
  Only accept the terms once your customer has agreed to them. If the terms change, the party has to accept the new version before the link is handed out again.
</Note>
