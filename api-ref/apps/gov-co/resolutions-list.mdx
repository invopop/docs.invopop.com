---
title: "List resolutions"
openapi: "GET /apps/gov-co/v1/entries/{silo_entry_id}/resolutions"
---

Lists the DIAN numbering resolutions linked to the supplier, with their prefix, authorized range, validity dates and technical key. Use the prefix as the invoice `series`, and pick codes within the range and validity dates.

<Note>
  Sandbox suppliers always return the fixed DIAN test resolution (series `SETP`). Production suppliers return their current resolutions, fetched live from the DIAN. Both also include any [uploaded contingency resolutions](/api-ref/apps/gov-co/resolutions-upload), which have no technical key. The silo entry must belong to the authenticated workspace and have been registered with the Colombia app first.
</Note>
