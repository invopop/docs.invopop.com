---
title: "List resolutions"
openapi: "GET /apps/gov-co/v1/entries/{silo_entry_id}/resolutions"
---

Lists the DIAN numbering resolutions linked to the supplier, with their prefix, authorized range, validity dates and technical key. Use the prefix as the invoice `series`, and pick codes within the range and validity dates.

<Note>
  Sandbox suppliers always return the fixed DIAN test resolution (series `SETP`). Production suppliers return their current resolutions, fetched live from the DIAN through Alegra. The silo entry must belong to the authenticated workspace and have been registered with the Colombia app first.
</Note>
