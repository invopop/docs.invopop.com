	<Accordion title="Stamp Duty Invoice (Marca da Bollo)">

		This example matches the **B2B invoice** above but adds a **€2.00 Marca da bollo** (stamp duty) as a document-level charge.

		**Flat-rate scheme (_forfettario_):** taxpayers using the _regime forfettario_ do **not** charge VAT on their invoices. When the document total goes **above €77.47**, they must account for **imposta di bollo**—typically by adding a **€2.00** stamp duty line, as in this sample. (Thresholds and rules can change; confirm with your adviser or the Agenzia delle Entrate if in doubt.)

		Notice:

		- the `charges` array includes an entry with `"key": "stamp-duty"`, amount `"2.00"`, and reason `Imposta di bollo`,
		- the minimal version is otherwise the same as the B2B example; `gobl build` recalculates totals including the charge,
		- the built version shows `totals.charge` and a higher `payable` / `total_with_tax` than the plain B2B invoice,
		- the [`it-sdi-v1`](https://docs.gobl.org/addons/it-sdi-v1) addon continues to validate the document for SDI transmission.

		Notice:

		- the SDI validates that exempt invoices include proper exemption codes before transmission.

	<Accordion title="Invoice after a documento commerciale">

		In this example, a retail sale was first recorded with a [Smart Receipt](/guides/it-ticket) (*documento commerciale*) and the customer later asked for an invoice. The invoice is an ordinary SDI invoice that references the receipt it replaces.

		Notice:

		- the line item carries an `attributes` entry with `type` `N.DOC.COMM`, the receipt's AdE document number in `text`, its progressive number in `amount`, and the receipt date in `date`,
		- the `it-sdi-v1` addon converts each item attribute into an `AltriDatiGestionali` block: `type` becomes `TipoDato`, `text` becomes `RiferimentoTesto`, `amount` becomes `RiferimentoNumero`, and `date` becomes `RiferimentoData`, which is the layout the AdE describes for invoices preceded by a receipt,
		- `type` is limited to 10 ASCII characters and `text` to 60 characters, and `amount` is rendered with at least two decimals, so `7816` arrives at SDI as `7816.00`,
		- the receipt's document number is the `ade-ref` stamp the Smart Receipts app adds to the receipt's header after the AdE accepts it,
		- `op_date` records the day of the sale while `issue_date` is the day the invoice is issued, and without further tags `gobl build` sets the document type to `TD01`,
		- the Smart Receipt itself is not changed by this invoice, and your own VAT records must remove the invoiced amount from that day's *corrispettivi*. See [Invoice requested after a receipt](/guides/it-ticket#invoice-requested-after-a-receipt) for the full sequence.
