	<Accordion title="Example B2C invoice">

		In this example, we're issuing a B2C simplified invoice from a Colombian supplier to a consumer.

		Notice:

		- we've set the `simplified` tag in the `tax.tags` field; this indicates the invoice is for a final consumer,
		- the customer information is omitted, as it's not required for B2C transactions; the DIAN receives it as _Consumidor final_, and no customer municipality code is needed,
		- the invoice includes a prepayment advance of 100%, meaning the invoice has been fully paid,
		- the totals section shows the `advance` and `due` amounts, with the latter being zero since the invoice is fully paid, and,
		- there are no calculations in some fields; these will be made automatically when uploading.

	<Accordion title="Example credit note">

		In this example, we're issuing a credit note (nota de crédito) that corrects a previously issued invoice.

		Notice:

		- the `type` is set to `credit-note`,
		- the `series` is set to a dedicated credit note series, separate from the invoice series,
		- the `preceding` array references the original invoice being corrected by its `series`, `code`, and `issue_date`, along with a `reason` for the correction,
		- the correction concept is set with the [`co-dian-credit-code`](https://docs.gobl.org/addons/co-dian-v2) extension on the preceding entry,
		- the lines describe the amounts being credited — a credit note can cover the full original invoice or just part of it, and,
		- there are no calculations in some fields; these will be made automatically when uploading.

	<Accordion title="Example debit note">

		In this example, we're issuing a debit note (nota de débito) that increases the amount of a previously cleared invoice.

		Notice:

		- the `type` is set to `debit-note`,
		- the `series` is set to a dedicated debit note series, separate from the invoice series; notes don't need a numbering resolution,
		- the `preceding` array references the original invoice by its `series`, `code`, and `issue_date`, along with a `reason` for the adjustment,
		- the preceding entry carries the `dian-cude` stamp of the original invoice, taken from its cleared envelope; the DIAN requires it for every debit note,
		- the adjustment concept is set with the [`co-dian-debit-code`](https://docs.gobl.org/addons/co-dian-v2) extension on the preceding entry,
		- the lines describe only the additional amount being charged, and,
		- there are no calculations in some fields; these will be made automatically when uploading.
