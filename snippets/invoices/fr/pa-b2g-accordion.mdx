	<Accordion title="PA B2G invoice (public buyer)">

		An invoice from a French company to a French public entity. It uses the same [`fr-ctc-flow2-v1`](https://docs.gobl.org/addons/fr-ctc-flow2-v1) add-on as a domestic B2B invoice, with the data Chorus Pro needs on top.

		Notice:

		- both parties give a `SIRET` identity, which the add-on tags with `iso-scheme-id` `0009` and from which it derives the `SIREN`,
		- the customer carries a `private-id` identity with the public entity's service code, which the add-on tags with scheme `0224`,
		- `ordering.purchases[0].code` holds the *numéro d'engagement juridique* and `ordering.contracts[0].code` the contract reference, each 50 characters or fewer,
		- the invoice number is 20 characters or fewer and the IBAN is written as 27 characters without spaces, as Chorus Pro requires.
