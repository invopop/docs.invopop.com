  <Accordion title="My GOBL document for a Colombian invoice fails to build because of the postal code">
    The DIAN expects a 5-digit [municipal code](https://www.dian.gov.co/atencionciudadano/formulariosinstructivos/Formularios/2007/Codigos_municipios_2007.pdf), not a 6-digit postal code, in the [`co-dian-municipality`](https://docs.gobl.org/addons/co-dian-v2) extension of the supplier and of customers identified by a Colombian NIT. For example, use `11001` for Bogotá.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="How does Invopop issue invoices in Colombia?">
    Through [Alegra](https://www.alegra.com), an authorized DIAN technology provider. The [send workflow](/guides/co-dian-invoicing) signs the GOBL envelope, clears it with the DIAN, stamps the CUFE and QR code, and generates a compliant PDF.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="What does a GOBL invoice need to be accepted by the DIAN?">
    Set `$schema` to `https://gobl.org/draft-0/bill/invoice`, the supplier's `tax_id.country` to `CO`, and include the supplier's DIAN municipality code via the [`co-dian-municipality`](https://docs.gobl.org/addons/co-dian-v2) extension in its `ext`. Customers identified by a Colombian NIT need the extension too; foreign customers and `simplified` B2C invoices don't (see [When is the municipality code required?](/guides/co-dian-invoicing#when-is-the-municipality-code-required)). See the [GOBL Colombia tax regime](https://github.com/invopop/gobl/blob/main/regimes/co/README.md) for the full field reference and examples.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Do foreign or consumer customers need a municipality code?">
    No. The [`co-dian-municipality`](https://docs.gobl.org/addons/co-dian-v2) extension is only required on customers identified by a Colombian NIT, that is, with `tax_id.country` set to `CO` and a `tax_id.code`. Foreign customers, `simplified` B2C invoices without a customer, and consumers identified only by a cédula build without it. Invopop can't derive the code from the NIT, so collect it for each Colombian business customer.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Which taxes can appear on a Colombian invoice?">
    VAT (IVA, 19% standard or 5% reduced), consumption taxes (IC, INC), the industry and commerce tax (ICA), and the retained taxes ReteIVA, ReteICA, and Retefuente. A single invoice may need to combine up to three value added and retained taxes, all supported by the [GOBL Colombia tax regime](https://docs.gobl.org/regimes/co#tax-categories).
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Which document types can I issue in Colombia?">
    Invoices, credit notes, and debit notes. Credit and debit notes reference the corrected invoice in the `preceding` array; debit notes require every preceding entry to carry the `dian-cude` stamp of the original invoice.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="What are the sandbox limitations for Colombia?">
    Only Alegra's NIT (`9005590882`) can be the supplier. Invoices use series `SETP` with codes `990000000`–`995000000` (shared across users, so avoid common codes) and today's date in Colombia.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Why was my invoice rejected for its series or number?">
    The series, code, and issue date must fall within an active DIAN numbering resolution. Resolutions are refreshed automatically, so a rejection usually means it expired or ran out and the supplier needs a new one from the DIAN.
  </Accordion>
