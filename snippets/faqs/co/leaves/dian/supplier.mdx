  <Accordion title="What does a supplier need before issuing invoices in Colombia?">
    The supplier must be registered with the DIAN with an active RUT (Registro Único Tributario), complete the DIAN habilitación, and hold a valid billing numbering range authorization. The [supplier registration guide](/guides/co-dian-registration) walks through each step.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="How do I register a supplier in Colombia?">
    Upload a GOBL party with the supplier's name, NIT (with verification digit), and email, then run the registration workflow. It publishes an enablement portal link under `registration-link` in the entry's meta; the supplier's legal representative completes the DIAN enablement there, and the workflow finishes once the DIAN accepts it. See the [supplier registration guide](/guides/co-dian-registration).
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="What is the DIAN habilitación?">
    A one-time DIAN certification required before issuing real invoices. The supplier submits a TestSetId from the DIAN portal through the hosted portal, Alegra sends the test documents, and the supplier is enabled once the DIAN accepts them.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Do I need to register suppliers in the sandbox?">
    Yes, but only Alegra's NIT (`9005590882`) can be registered. The enablement is simulated: enter the TestSetId `a70562e0-631e-4ceb-aa65-36887b57dc17` for an instant acceptance. Other outcomes are in the [sandbox section](/guides/co-dian-registration#sandbox) of the guide.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Does the billing numbering range authorization expire?">
    Yes, both by number limit and by date. Before a range with unused numbers expires, the supplier can extend it ([extension request](https://www.dian.gov.co/impuestos/factura-electronica/Documents/Numeracion2022-Habilitacion-F.pdf)); once it's used up, they request a new one ([new authorization](https://www.dian.gov.co/impuestos/factura-electronica/Documents/Numeracion2022-AutorizacionF.pdf)). Updated resolutions are picked up automatically.
  </Accordion>
