  <Accordion title="What does a supplier need before issuing invoices in Colombia?">
    The supplier must be registered with the DIAN with an active RUT (Registro Único Tributario), complete the DIAN habilitación, and hold a valid billing numbering range authorization. The [supplier registration guide](/guides/co-dian-registration) walks through each step.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="How do I register a supplier in Colombia?">
    Upload a GOBL party with the supplier's name, NIT (including the verification digit), and email, then run the registration workflow. It creates the supplier's Alegra company and publishes a habilitación portal link in the entry's meta section. The supplier completes the habilitación from that portal, and the workflow finishes once the DIAN accepts the test set. See the [supplier registration guide](/guides/co-dian-registration) for the full walkthrough.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="What is the DIAN habilitación?">
    A one-time certification the DIAN requires before a supplier can issue production invoices. The supplier obtains a TestSetId from the DIAN habilitación portal and submits it through the hosted portal; Alegra then emits a set of test documents to the DIAN on the supplier's behalf. Once the DIAN accepts the test set, the supplier is enabled to issue real invoices.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Do I need to register suppliers in the sandbox?">
    Yes. The registration workflow runs in the sandbox too, but only Alegra's own NIT (`9005590882`) can be registered, since the DIAN test registry accepts documents from that emitter alone. The fixed DIAN test resolution (`SETP`) is seeded automatically on registration, and the habilitación is simulated: enter the TestSetId `a70562e0-631e-4ceb-aa65-36887b57dc17` in the portal for an instant acceptance. See the [supplier registration guide](/guides/co-dian-registration#sandbox) for the values that simulate rejected, pending, and failed outcomes.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Does the billing numbering range authorization expire?">
    Yes. DIAN numbering range authorizations have both a number limit and an expiration date. When one runs out, the supplier's legal representative must file a renewal with the DIAN ([Solicitud de Habilitación de Numeración de Facturación](https://www.dian.gov.co/impuestos/factura-electronica/Documents/Numeracion2022-Habilitacion-F.pdf)); the new resolution is picked up automatically before the next send.
  </Accordion>
