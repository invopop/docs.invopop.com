  <Accordion title="Can I invoice a Finnish business that isn't reachable on Peppol?">
    Yes. Most Finnish businesses receive e-invoices through the domestic operator network, and the [Finland app](/apps/finland) delivers there. You send one GOBL invoice with the receiver's e-invoice address, and it arrives in the format the receiver's operator expects. You can check which network a receiver is on at [verkkolaskuosoite.fi](https://verkkolaskuosoite.fi). For a receiver only on Peppol, use the [Peppol app](/apps/peppol).
  </Accordion>
