  <Accordion title="Which format should I use to invoice a Finnish public sector buyer?">
    Send a GOBL invoice through the [Finland app](/apps/finland). It converts the invoice to Peppol BIS Billing 3.0 (UBL 2.1) — the mandatory channel for Finnish public authorities, routed through Valtiokonttori (the Finnish State Treasury), Finland's national Peppol Authority — and delivers it through the Apix operator network.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Can I invoice a Finnish business that isn't reachable on Peppol?">
    Yes. Most Finnish businesses receive e-invoices through the domestic operator network rather than Peppol, and the [Finland app](/apps/finland) reaches both: you send one GOBL invoice with the receiver's e-invoice address, and the operator converts it to Finvoice for domestic-network receivers. There is no need to check which network the receiver is on.
  </Accordion>
