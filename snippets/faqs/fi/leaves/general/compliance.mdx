  <Accordion title="Is e-invoicing mandatory in Finland?">
    For public buyers, yes. Under Act 241/2019, central government has received e-invoices since 1 April 2019, and all other contracting entities since 1 April 2020. Since 1 April 2021, the state receives only invoices that follow EN 16931. Between businesses there is no transmission mandate, but a business can ask its supplier for e-invoices. There is no B2C mandate.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="What is Finland's B2B 'right to request' e-invoicing?">
    Since 1 April 2020, a public buyer or a business can ask another public buyer or business for its invoices as e-invoices. The act defines a business as one with an annual turnover above EUR 10,000. The rule applies to both sides, so a supplier at or below that turnover does not have to issue an e-invoice on request. The right does not apply to private persons.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Which Peppol ID does a Finnish company use?">
    Its OVT code with the scheme `0216`, for example `0216:003712345678`. The OVT code is `0037`, the 8-digit Business ID without the hyphen, and an optional suffix of up to 5 characters. The Finnish Peppol Authority allows no other scheme for Finnish organisations.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="What VAT rates apply in Finland?">
    The general rate is 25.5%. The reduced rate of 13.5% applies to food, restaurant services, books, medicines, passenger transport, accommodation and events, since 1 January 2026. The reduced rate of 10% applies to newspapers and magazines. Intra-EU supplies of goods to VAT-registered buyers and exports are zero-rated.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="How long must invoices be archived in Finland?">
    The Accounting Act (*Kirjanpitolaki*, 1336/1997) sets the periods. Invoices and other vouchers must be kept for at least 6 years from the end of the year in which the financial year ended. Financial statements must be kept for at least 10 years. Invoices must stay authentic, complete and readable for the whole period. Invoices about investments in real estate must be kept for 13 years.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Does an invoice need an electronic signature in Finland?">
    No. The supplier and the buyer can choose any control process that gives a reliable audit trail between the invoice and the supply.
  </Accordion>
