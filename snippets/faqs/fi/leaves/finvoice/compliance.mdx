<Accordion title="What is Finvoice?">
  Finvoice is the Finnish XML e-invoicing format, published by Finance Finland (*Finanssiala*), the association of Finnish banks. Version 3.0 can carry EN 16931 content, and it is a main format on Finland's domestic operator network. You do not produce Finvoice documents yourself. Send a GOBL invoice, and the receiver gets it in the format its operator expects.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="What are TEAPPSXML and the e-invoice address?">
  TEAPPSXML is the other national e-invoice format, owned by Tieto and used between operators. To reach a company on the domestic network, you need two values from the national register at [verkkolaskuosoite.fi](https://verkkolaskuosoite.fi). The e-invoice address is usually the company's OVT code. The operator ID names the operator or bank that receives for the company.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="Do I need to choose between the domestic network and Peppol?">
  Yes, each network has its own app. Finland runs two e-invoicing networks side by side. The domestic operator network carries most invoices, and its address register lists about 370,000 Business IDs. Peppol is the second network. The [Finland app](/apps/finland) delivers on the domestic network, and the [Peppol app](/apps/peppol) on Peppol. To see which app a receiver needs, look them up at [verkkolaskuosoite.fi](https://verkkolaskuosoite.fi), which lists addresses on both networks.
</Accordion>
