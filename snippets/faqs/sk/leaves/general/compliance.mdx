  <Accordion title="Is e-invoicing mandatory in Slovakia?">
    Yes, from 1 January 2027. VAT payers established in Slovakia must issue e-invoices for domestic sales to businesses (B2B) and public bodies (B2G), and every business must be able to receive them. During 2026, e-invoicing is voluntary.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Who must be able to receive e-invoices?">
    Every legal entity and taxable person in Slovakia, including sole traders, the self-employed, farmers and people who rent out property. In practice, each of them needs a contract with a digital postman.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Does it apply to consumer (B2C) sales?">
    No. The mandate currently covers only sales between businesses (B2B) and between businesses and public bodies (B2G).
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Does the mandate apply to foreign companies?">
    Not until mid-2030. A company registered for VAT in Slovakia but not established there does not have to issue or receive e-invoices through a digital postman until 30 June 2030. Cross-border transactions come into scope from 1 July 2030.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Which transactions don't need an e-invoice?">
    Simplified invoices (receipts up to EUR 100 and eKasa receipts up to EUR 400 including VAT), VAT-exempt supplies, and supplies between members of the same VAT group. Classified supplies, for example to the intelligence services, must not be issued as e-invoices.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Do I need my customer's consent to send an e-invoice?">
    No. When an e-invoice is mandatory, the recipient's consent is not required.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="What is a digital postman?">
    A certified provider that sends and receives e-invoices over Peppol for a business and reports the invoice data to the Financial Administration. Providers must be accredited by the Financial Administration, which publishes the list. Each business chooses its postman on the Financial Administration's portal.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Do I still file the VAT control statement?">
    Yes, until 30 June 2030. From 1 July 2030, the control statement and the summary statement are abolished, and invoice data reaches the Financial Administration automatically through the digital postmen.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="How long must e-invoices be kept in Slovakia?">
    For 10 years from the end of the calendar year they relate to, in their original XML form. This is the business's obligation: digital postmen don't have to keep the invoices they transmit.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="What are the penalties?">
    Up to EUR 10,000 for invoice data that is not reported, reported incorrectly or reported late, and up to EUR 100,000 for repeated violations. An obvious mistake that is corrected promptly, or a proven failure of your digital postman, is not fined.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="What VAT rates apply in Slovakia?">
    A standard rate of 23%, with reduced rates of 19% and 5%, all in force since 1 January 2025. Slovakia uses the euro.
  </Accordion>
