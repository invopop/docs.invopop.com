  <Accordion title="What does a party need to register with RO e-Factura?">
    A Romanian tax ID (CIF) and a qualified digital certificate of a person with access to the company's SPV (*Spațiul Privat Virtual*) as legal representative or authorized person. The certificate holder opens the registration link, accepts the terms and authorizes Invopop on ANAF's site. Invopop never sees or stores the certificate.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Can one person register several companies?">
    Yes. An accountant or representative with SPV rights for several companies can use the same certificate for all of them, but each company is a separate party in Invopop. Run the registration workflow and complete the authorization once per party.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Where do I find the registration link?">
    After the **Register with ANAF** step runs, the link is stored in the **Meta** section of the party entry under the `link` key. It opens the terms first and then continues to ANAF. The workflow waits on the **Wait ANAF link registration** step until the certificate holder completes the authorization, and the link is removed once the party is registered.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Can I show the terms in my own interface?">
    Yes. If you register parties for your own customers, fetch the terms with `GET /apps/gov-ro/v1/entry/{silo_entry_id}/terms`, show them in your own interface and record your customer's acceptance with `POST` on the same path. The response carries the ANAF link to send to the certificate holder. See the [white label registration](/guides/ro-efactura-supplier#white-label-registration) flow.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Where is the acceptance of the terms stored?">
    On the party entry, as a private agreement file named `terms-and-conditions.html` holding the exact terms accepted. Its meta records the version, the acceptance time and whether they were accepted on the registration page or through the API.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Why did my party registration fail?">
    Common reasons:

    - **Wrong certificate**: the certificate chosen in the browser does not belong to a person with SPV rights for the company's CIF.
    - **SPV rights missing**: the certificate holder is not registered in SPV as legal representative or authorized person of the company.
    - **Party not Romanian**: the party's `tax_id` country must be `RO`.
    - **Terms not accepted**: the ANAF authorization only goes through once the terms have been accepted.

    After fixing the cause, open the same registration link again.

    <Note>
      The page shown at the end of the authorization tells whether it succeeded, and shows the error otherwise. The workflow error logs in the Invopop Console have more detail.
    </Note>
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="How long does an ANAF authorization last?">
    One year. Invopop renews access automatically during that year, after which the party has to complete the registration again. Notifications before the deadline are in development and will soon be supported through the app configuration.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Can I test with the sandbox?">
    Yes. In an Invopop sandbox workspace, every call goes to ANAF's test environment, so invoices sent there have no fiscal value. The registration still uses a real digital certificate, since ANAF authenticates the same way in both environments.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="How do I stop sending and receiving for a party?">
    Run the **RO unregister party** workflow on the party. It removes the ANAF authorization from Invopop and unsubscribes the party from periodic sync. To receive again, run the registration workflow again.
  </Accordion>
