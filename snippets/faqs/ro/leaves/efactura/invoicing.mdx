  <Accordion title="Which GOBL documents can I send to RO e-Factura?">
    Invoices that declare the `ro-efactura-v1` addon: standard invoices (380), invoices for accounting purposes (751, with the `accounting` tag), self-billed invoices (389, with the `self-billed` tag), credit notes (381) and corrective invoices (384).
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="How long does ANAF take to accept an invoice?">
    Usually a few minutes. After the upload, the **Send e-Factura XML to ANAF** step waits and checks the upload status until ANAF answers `ok` or `nok`. The job finishes when ANAF has decided.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Where do I find the official invoice?">
    When ANAF accepts the invoice, Invopop attaches the official XML returned by ANAF to the invoice entry as `anaf-efactura.xml`, next to the generated `efactura.xml`. The archive is also kept in Invopop's database.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="What happens when ANAF rejects an invoice?">
    The job fails with the errors ANAF returned. Correct the invoice and run the workflow again; a rejected invoice can be sent again.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Can I send the same invoice twice?">
    No. Invopop never uploads an accepted invoice twice. To change an accepted invoice, issue a credit note or a corrective invoice that references it.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="How are B2C and self-billed invoices handled?">
    Invopop picks the ANAF upload options from the invoice itself. An invoice to a customer without a tax ID goes to the B2C endpoint, and a self-billed invoice is uploaded with the customer's CIF and flagged as `autofactura`. No workflow configuration is needed.
  </Accordion>
