  <Accordion title="Is e-invoicing mandatory in Romania?">
    Yes. Taxable persons established in Romania must send their domestic invoices to RO e-Factura. It is mandatory for invoices to public bodies (B2G) since July 2022 and for invoices to businesses (B2B) since July 2024. Invoices to consumers (B2C) must be reported since January 2025.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Is RO e-Factura part of the Peppol network?">
    No. RO e-Factura is a national clearance platform that the Ministry of Finance and ANAF run. Every domestic invoice goes to this one platform through its API or through SPV. There is no Peppol directory and no network of access points in the domestic flow.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="What is the deadline to send an invoice to RO e-Factura?">
    5 working days from the issue date, since 1 January 2026. Before that date, the deadline was 5 calendar days. The supplier must issue the invoice by the 15th of the month after the supply.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Does RO e-Factura apply to consumer (B2C) sales?">
    Yes, when the supplier issues an invoice to the consumer. The supplier reports the invoice to RO e-Factura and gives it to the consumer in the usual way. A fiscal cash register receipt (bon fiscal) that meets the conditions of a simplified invoice does not go to RO e-Factura.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="What is the difference between a bon fiscal and a B2C invoice?">
    A B2C invoice (*factură*) is an invoice that the supplier issues to a consumer. The supplier reports it to RO e-Factura and gives it to the consumer. A bon fiscal is a receipt from a fiscal cash register, under OUG 28/1999. Its data reaches ANAF through the cash register, not through RO e-Factura. A bon fiscal that meets the conditions of a simplified invoice does not go to RO e-Factura.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Which transactions are out of scope?">
    Exports and intra-Community supplies are out of scope. Qualifying fiscal cash register receipts are also out of scope. Since January 2026, invoices to buyers that are registered for VAT in Romania but not established there are in scope.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Does a foreign provider need a certification to connect?">
    No. Romania has no certification scheme for e-invoicing providers. A provider uses a qualified certificate for electronic signature. Each business authorizes the provider in SPV to act for its tax number (CIF).
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="What are the penalties?">
    A late transmission costs RON 1,000 to 10,000, based on the size of the taxpayer. An invoice issued outside the system costs the supplier 15% of its value. A buyer that books an invoice from outside the system also pays 15% of its value and cannot deduct the VAT.
  </Accordion>
