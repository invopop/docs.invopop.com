  <Accordion title="What format does a Romanian e-invoice use?">
    UBL 2.1 in the Romanian profile of EN 16931, called CIUS-RO (version 1.0.1). RO e-Factura also accepts CII (D16B). The platform checks the invoice against the EN 16931 rules and the national BR-RO rules.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Is a PDF invoice still valid?">
    No. The legal invoice is the XML file that the Ministry of Finance seals after validation. A PDF has no legal value, but you can give one to your customer as a readable copy.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="How do I correct an invoice after RO e-Factura accepts it?">
    You cannot cancel a sealed invoice. Issue a credit note (type 381) or a corrective invoice (type 384) that references the original invoice.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Can the buyer reject an invoice in RO e-Factura?">
    No. The platform statuses show only whether the invoice passed validation. The buyer settles a dispute directly with the supplier, and the supplier corrects the invoice with a new document.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Which VAT rates apply in Romania?">
    Since 1 August 2025, the standard rate is 21% and the reduced rate is 11%. If an invoice is in another currency, the VAT amounts must also show in RON.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="How long can I download invoices from RO e-Factura?">
    For 60 days. After that, ANAF archives the files, and a copy is available only on request. Keep your own copy of each sealed invoice.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Is self-billing allowed?">
    Yes. The buyer issues the invoice in the supplier's name, with type code 389.
  </Accordion>
