  <Accordion title="How do I register a supplier with VERI*FACTU?">
    Run the VERI\*FACTU Register Supplier workflow. The legal representative signs the PDF agreement (electronic or handwritten signature with ID) and Invopop registers the supplier with AEAT for invoicing on their behalf.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="What should I do if a supplier needs to update their details, such as their address, after registration?">
    Once a supplier is registered, it's their tax ID that drives their issuing entitlements. Even a name change requires no further action, as the agreement remains valid for that tax ID (the entity). The exception is a change of tax ID: in that case, create a new supplier and unregister the old one, as if they were a new client.

    So, in the case of an address change, update the details on your end and issue invoices with the new data. The AEAT does not validate the address when invoices are submitted and a change of address does not invalidate the registration agreement. The address is only required at the moment of registration, and should be up to date then.

    <Warning>
      For autónomos (self-employed individuals), block name changes after registration. The AEAT rejects invoices where the name doesn't match the tax ID of an autónomo.
    </Warning>
  </Accordion>
