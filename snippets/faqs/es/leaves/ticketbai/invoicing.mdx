  <Accordion title="What's the workflow for issuing TicketBAI invoices?">
    Build a GOBL invoice with the `es-tbai-v1` addon, send it through the TicketBAI Issue Invoice workflow. Invopop signs with its *sello de empresa* certificate, generates the chained XML, and submits to the relevant Diputación Foral (Bizkaia, Gipuzkoa, or Álava).
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Why don't TicketBAI QR codes work in the sandbox environment?">
    This is expected. QR codes generated in the TicketBAI sandbox environment are intentionally non-functional to prevent sandbox invoices from being mistaken for real ones.

    To verify that a sandbox QR code is correct, upload the PDF or a QR image to the foral tax authority's test verification page: [pruebatuz.bizkaia.eus](https://pruebatuz.bizkaia.eus/B4PEQR0C/testQr.xhtml?idioma=es).
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="What are the most common TicketBAI errors and how do I fix them?">
    These are the errors we see most often when submitting invoices to the Diputaciones Forales:

    | Code | Error | Meaning and fix |
    |---|---|---|
    | `B4_2000001` / `B4_2000002` | XML fails XSD validation | Typically an invoice `code` longer than 20 characters. Some are `cvc-pattern` failures, meaning a field doesn't match the expected format. Fix the offending field and resend in a new document. |
    | `N3_0000002` | Supplier not identified, or legal name incorrect | The NIF or legal name isn't registered with that region's tax agency, often after a change of legal name. Update the supplier's details with the agency, then resend. |
    | `503` | Service unavailable | The agency's service is temporarily down. Retry later. |
    | `B4_2000016` | Credit note has no series | Credit notes need a non-empty `series`. Add one and resend. |
    | `008` | Signature verification error | Usually reported after the fact by the Diputación Foral, even though the invoice was accepted at submission. It's often caused on the tax agency's side. Issue a new invoice rather than trying to correct the original. |
    | `004` / `012` | Software registration verification error | The supplier's TicketBAI registration isn't complete, for example when the final onboarding confirmation hasn't been done. Finish the [supplier registration](/guides/es-ticketbai-supplier) before issuing invoices. |

    Because TicketBAI documents are chained, a rejected invoice can't be edited in place. Correct the problem in a new document and send it again.
  </Accordion>
