  <Accordion title="How do I onboard a new supplier in Germany?">
    Issuing XRechnung or ZUGFeRD files needs no registration. To send and receive through Peppol, follow the [supplier registration guide](/guides/de-registration): the supplier's legal representative completes an onboarding, and Invopop then registers the supplier on Peppol.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="How do I register a supplier with Peppol in Germany?">
    Run the **Germany register supplier** workflow. It adds the supplier's GEBA (scheme `0246`, its VAT ID with the `DE` prefix) as its Peppol ID when the party has none, and publishes an onboarding link. Once the onboarding is approved, Invopop registers the supplier on Peppol.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Can two suppliers in my workspace share a VAT ID?">
    No. While one supplier of the workspace is in onboarding or registered with a VAT ID, registration refuses that VAT ID for another supplier. Unregister the first supplier to register the VAT ID again.
  </Accordion>
