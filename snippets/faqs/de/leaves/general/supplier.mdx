  <Accordion title="How do I onboard a new supplier in Germany?">
    Register every supplier with the [supplier registration guide](/guides/de-registration), whether it uses email or Peppol. The supplier's legal representative completes an onboarding, and Invopop then registers the supplier on Peppol.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="How do I register a supplier with Peppol in Germany?">
    Run the **Germany register supplier** workflow. It adds the supplier's GEBA (scheme `0246`, its VAT ID with the `DE` prefix) as its Peppol ID when the party has none, and publishes an onboarding link. Once the onboarding is approved, Invopop registers the supplier on Peppol.
  </Accordion>
