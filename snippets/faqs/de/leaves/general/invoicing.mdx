  <Accordion title="How do I configure my workspace for German invoicing?">
    Pick a format based on the recipient: XRechnung (UBL or CII) for B2G via Peppol, ZUGFeRD/Factur-X for B2B where a human-readable PDF is also wanted. Both are EN 16931-compliant, and Invopop generates either from the same GOBL invoice.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Can I send German e-invoices by email?">
    Yes. The **Germany send invoice** workflow sends the XRechnung XML by email when the customer is not on Peppol, and the **ZUGFeRD email invoice** workflow sends a ZUGFeRD PDF. [Register the supplier](/guides/de-registration) first, as for Peppol. See the [invoicing guide](/guides/de-invoicing).
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="What's the workflow for issuing Peppol invoices in Germany?">
    [Register the supplier](/guides/de-registration), then use the **Germany send invoice** workflow of the [invoicing guide](/guides/de-invoicing). It sends the XRechnung through Peppol when the customer is on the network and accepts XRechnung there, or by email otherwise. ZUGFeRD invoices go by email.
  </Accordion>

    <Accordion title="Which XRechnung syntax should I choose, UBL or CII?">
    Both syntaxes are equally valid and contain the same information, so in the majority of cases the choice has no consequences.

    If you plan on sending invoices through the Peppol network, we recommend using UBL as it is the supported format. If you plan on also issuing invoices in ZUGFeRD or Factur-X, it might be more convenient to use CII, as it is the syntax also supported by ZUGFeRD and Factur-X.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="How can I view an XML attached to a PDF?">
    For ZUGFeRD PDFs, the XML file is embedded within the PDF itself. To extract and view it, you can use the `Attachments` section in Adobe Acrobat Reader.

    Alternatively, you can use specialized tools like the [SysTools PDF Extractor](https://www.systoolsgroup.com/pdf/extractor/) to extract the XML file.
  </Accordion>


  <Accordion title="Where do I find Germany-specific GOBL documentation?">
    See the [Germany tax regime in GOBL](https://docs.gobl.org/regimes/de) for tax categories and identifiers. The XRechnung rules live in [`de-xrechnung-v3`](https://docs.gobl.org/addons/de-xrechnung-v3) and the ZUGFeRD rules in [`de-zugferd-v2`](https://docs.gobl.org/addons/de-zugferd-v2). For Peppol, see the [`eu-en16931-v2017`](https://docs.gobl.org/addons/eu-en16931-v2017) addon and the [Peppol app reference](/apps/peppol).
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="What if the customer is not on the Peppol network?">
    The **Germany send invoice** workflow sends the XRechnung to the customer's email address instead. Peppol is not mandatory in Germany, but the invoice must still be an EN 16931 e-invoice. See the [invoicing guide](/guides/de-invoicing).
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="How do I handle 'Receiver Not Found' errors?">
    A **Send Peppol document** step fails with `KO` and `receiver not found in the peppol network` when the customer's Peppol ID is not on the network. Check the customer's Peppol ID, or send the invoice by email. The **Germany send invoice** workflow avoids this error: its **Lookup Peppol participant** step finds the Peppol ID from the customer's VAT ID, and its **Verify document can be sent** step sends by email when the customer is not on Peppol.
  </Accordion>
