  <Accordion title="How do I receive e-invoices by email in Germany?">
    Create a [Connect](/apps/connect) email endpoint and give its address to your suppliers. Connect imports each XRechnung or ZUGFeRD invoice that it receives as a GOBL invoice. See the [receiving guide](/guides/de-receiving).
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Do I need to register the supplier to receive e-invoices?">
    Yes. Register every supplier with the [supplier registration guide](/guides/de-registration), on both channels. The registration also registers the supplier on Peppol, so it can receive through Peppol.
  </Accordion>
