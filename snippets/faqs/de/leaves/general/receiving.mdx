  <Accordion title="How do I receive e-invoices by email in Germany?">
    Register the supplier with the [supplier registration guide](/guides/de-registration) first. Then register it for email with the [Email](/apps/email) app, which gives the supplier an `invopop.email` address. The Email app imports each XRechnung or ZUGFeRD invoice sent there as a GOBL invoice. See the [receiving guide](/guides/de-receiving).
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Do I need to register the supplier to receive e-invoices?">
    Yes. Register every supplier first with the [supplier registration guide](/guides/de-registration). The registration also registers the supplier on Peppol, so it can receive through Peppol. To receive by email, also register the supplier with the Email app, which gives it its address.
  </Accordion>
