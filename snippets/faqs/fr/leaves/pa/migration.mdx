  <Accordion title="How does the 5-day deadline work?">
    The company has 5 business days, to answer, counted from the moment Invopop receives the request and ending at the same time of day (Paris time). For example, a request received on Friday at 15:00 expires the following Friday at 15:00. French public holidays are not taken into account. The form shows the date to answer by.

    Until then, **Wait for migration response** stays queued, and the workflow continues as soon as the company answers. If the address is still unanswered at the deadline, Invopop accepts it automatically: on the date the new PA requested when that is still possible, otherwise on the earliest possible date. Once the response is sent, the answer can't be changed.

    The deadline only applies to workspaces with a migration workflow configured.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Which effective dates can the company choose?">
    The form's calendar only offers dates from the earliest the switch can happen: at least 3 business days and 4 calendar days from the answer, whichever is later. Business days are Monday to Friday.

    If the company accepts without choosing a date, the date the new PA requested is used. If that date is already too soon, the earliest possible date is used instead, and the answer is sent as `ACWC`.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="When is a migration request complete?">
    An incoming request can cover several addresses, and each address gets its own job and form. Each answer is sent to the new PA as soon as it's given, with the addresses not answered yet marked pending.

    The request is complete once every address in it has been answered and the last answer has been sent. Until then, it shows as **Received** in the [Migrations tab](/guides/fr-pa-migrations#reading-migrations).
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="What does the Reason in the Migrations tab mean?">
    When an address was refused, the side panel of the [Migrations tab](/guides/fr-pa-migrations#reading-migrations) shows the Reason next to the Decision. `REFUS_CLIENT` means the company refused the takeover, and `ADRESSE_INCONNUE` means the address isn't held at Invopop. Reasons from other platforms appear as they sent them.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="What happens if I don't have a migration workflow configured?">
    Invopop receives and records the request, but no workflow job starts for your workspace's addresses: there is no form link, no automatic acceptance at the deadline, and no `MOBILITY_RESPONSE` is sent to the other PA.

    To answer it, add the [PPF incoming migration](/guides/fr-pa-migrations#setup) workflow and select it in the France app configuration, then contact Invopop support to have the pending request processed again. The 5-day deadline still counts from when the request first arrived, so if it has already passed, the address is accepted automatically as soon as the workflow runs.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Does the 'Start Peppol migration' action always have to run?">
    No, it's optional. Run it on the `accepted` branch when the party is registered on Peppol: its migration key is passed to the new PA, which uses it to move the party's Peppol registration.

    Without it, the response carries `NEANT`, meaning there is nothing to migrate on Peppol. The key is never sent for refused addresses.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="What outcomes does the 'Wait for migration response' action have?">
    The step stays queued until the company answers or the deadline passes, then ends with one of these codes:

    - `accepted`: the address was accepted, on the requested date or another one, either by the company or automatically at the deadline.
    - `rejected`: the company refused the takeover. The response is sent as `RJCT` with motif `REFUS_CLIENT`.

    Both outcomes continue to **Complete incoming migration**, which sends the answer to the other PA. A typical workflow runs **Start Peppol migration** only on the `accepted` branch.
  </Accordion>
