  <Accordion title="How do I register a supplier with Smart Receipts?">
    Create the supplier party with its Partita IVA and run the Smart Receipts Register Supplier workflow. The workflow returns a signed registration link in the silo entry's meta. Open the link (or share it with the supplier) and submit the supplier's AdE credentials. Once the form is accepted, the post-registration workflow runs and the supplier can start issuing receipts. See the [supplier registration guide](/guides/it-ticket-supplier) for the full steps.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="What credentials does Smart Receipts require to authenticate a supplier?">
    Four values: the supplier's Partita IVA, plus the Fisconline login (personal *codice fiscale*, password and PIN) of a person authorised to act for the supplier at ivaservizi.agenziaentrate.gov.it. The Partita IVA says which business the receipts are filed under; the login says whose credentials are used to send them. The *codice fiscale* is the same "tax code" that person types to log in to the AdE portal, so it is their own 16-character code, not the company's. They are submitted through the registration link and passed securely to our local partner, who uses them to transmit receipts to the AdE on the supplier's behalf. Invopop stores the *codice fiscale* on the party but never stores the password or PIN. See the [supplier registration guide](/guides/it-ticket-supplier#prerequisites) for details.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Whose AdE credentials can be used to register a company with Smart Receipts?">
    Anyone the company has appointed in AdE as *incaricato*, either *gestore incaricato* or *operatore incaricato*. That can be the legal representative, an employee or the company's accountant; it doesn't need to be the owner. A *delega* isn't accepted for Smart Receipts, so an accountant must be appointed as *incaricato* instead, and SPID or CIE logins aren't supported. Before submitting, check that the person can log in to *Fatture e Corrispettivi*, choose **Incaricato**, select the company and open *Corrispettivi*. If the appointment isn't active yet, the form is still accepted but receipts fail with `invalid-credentials` until the credentials are submitted again. If one person covers several suppliers, their credentials are stored separately for each one, so after the 90-day password change they must resubmit them in every supplier's registration form.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="How often do the supplier's AdE credentials need to be renewed?">
    Every 90 days. The supplier must first renew the credentials on the AdE portal, then resubmit them through the same registration link (or via the API `PUT` to the link) so receipt issuance is not interrupted. If they aren't renewed in time, issuing fails with the fault code `invalid-credentials` until the supplier resubmits them.
  </Accordion>
