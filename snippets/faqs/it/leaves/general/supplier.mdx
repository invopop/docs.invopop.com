  <Accordion title="How do I onboard a new supplier in Italy?">
    Provide the supplier's Partita IVA and Codice Fiscale (where different) in their party configuration. No certificate upload is needed — Invopop transmits to SDI over its own accredited channel.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="How are supplier credentials stored in Invopop for Italy?">
    No supplier certificates are required. Invopop exchanges documents with SDI over its own accredited channel; only the Partita IVA and routing metadata are stored.
  </Accordion>
