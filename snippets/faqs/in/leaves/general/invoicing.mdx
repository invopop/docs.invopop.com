  <Accordion title="Which format does an Indian e-invoice use?">
    JSON in the FORM GST INV-01 schema, which is part of the CGST Rules. Version 1.1 applies today. Version 1.2 applies from 1 January 2027.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="How long do I have to report an invoice to the IRP?">
    If your aggregate annual turnover is Rs 10 crore or more, the IRP refuses invoices, credit notes and debit notes older than 30 days. Below Rs 10 crore there is no such limit today.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Can I cancel an e-invoice?">
    Yes, within 24 hours of IRN generation. You cancel the whole document, never part of it. If an e-way bill exists for the IRN, cancel the e-way bill first. After 24 hours, issue a credit note or a debit note.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Can I send the invoice as a PDF?">
    Yes. The IRP does not deliver the invoice, so you send it to the buyer by any channel. If you are in scope, the invoice must carry the IRN and the QR code that the IRP returns.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Do I still file GSTR-1?">
    Yes. The IRP sends the data of each reported document to the GST system, and it fills your GSTR-1 return. You still check and file the return.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="What is the e-way bill and how does it relate to the e-invoice?">
    The e-way bill is a separate document for the movement of goods when the consignment value exceeds Rs 50,000. All six IRPs can make the e-way bill from the IRN data. From 1 January 2027 the e-way bill number is planned to grow from 12 to 14 digits.
  </Accordion>
