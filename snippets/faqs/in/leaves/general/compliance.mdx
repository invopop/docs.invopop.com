  <Accordion title="Is e-invoicing mandatory in India?">
    Yes, for registered persons whose aggregate turnover exceeds Rs 5 crore in any preceding financial year from 2017-18. They get an Invoice Reference Number (IRN) from an Invoice Registration Portal (IRP) for each B2B, B2G and export document. The threshold applies since 1 August 2023.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="How is the Rs 5 crore turnover calculated?">
    The test uses the aggregate turnover of the PAN. All GST registrations (GSTINs) of the same PAN count together, in any preceding financial year from 2017-18. If the turnover exceeds Rs 5 crore in one year, GSTN expects e-invoicing from the next financial year.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="What is an IRN?">
    The Invoice Reference Number is a unique 64-character reference that the IRP gives to each reported document. It is a hash of the supplier GSTIN, the financial year, the document type and the document number. An invoice that a supplier in scope issues without an IRN is not a valid invoice.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Which portals can I use to get an IRN?">
    Six IRPs are live: IRP-1 and IRP-2, which NIC runs, and the private IRPs of Cygnet (IRP-3), Clear (IRP-4), EY (IRP-5) and IRIS (IRP-6). You can use any of them. Reporting and IRN generation are free on all six.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Does e-invoicing apply to B2C sales?">
    No. Invoices to consumers get no IRN. A supplier with turnover above Rs 500 crore must print a dynamic QR code on B2C invoices. The customer can scan it to pay.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Who is exempt from e-invoicing?">
    Insurers, banks, financial institutions and NBFCs, goods transport agencies, passenger transport services, multiplex cinemas, Special Economic Zone units, government departments and local authorities. These suppliers are exempt whatever their turnover.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="What changes on 1 January 2027?">
    The buyer must also get an IRN for its own reverse-charge self-invoices. This covers purchases from unregistered suppliers where the buyer pays the tax under reverse charge, and imports of services from suppliers outside India. The INV-01 schema moves to version 1.2 with the new supply types `RCMDOM` and `RCMIMP`. The law applies from 1 January 2027, but GSTN has not yet announced the production date on the IRPs.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Does e-invoicing apply to a foreign supplier?">
    A foreign supplier that is not registered for GST in India is not in scope. From 1 January 2027, an Indian buyer in scope reports the self-invoice for imported services (`RCMIMP`) instead.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="How long must I keep invoices?">
    A registered person keeps its books and records for 72 months from the due date of the annual return for the year. The e-invoice portals keep e-invoices for download for six months only.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Does Invopop support e-invoicing in India?">
    Not today. Invopop does not connect to the IRPs. For inquiries, contact us at [support@invopop.com](mailto:support@invopop.com).
  </Accordion>
