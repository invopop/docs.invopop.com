  <Accordion title="Italian business">

    Put the *partita IVA* in `tax_id`, and the *codice destinatario* in an `it-sdi-code` inbox. SDI uses that code to deliver the invoice. A customer who receives by certified email (PEC) gets an `it-sdi-pec` inbox with their PEC address instead. If you have both, add both: SDI delivers to the *codice destinatario*, and the PEC is left out of the file.

  <Accordion title="Italian private individual">

    Put their *codice fiscale* in `identities`, with the key `it-fiscal-code`. They have no VAT number, so `tax_id` holds only the country. Leave out inboxes: SDI files the invoice in the customer's personal area on the tax authority's portal, so send them a copy yourself, normally as a PDF.

  <Accordion title="Customer outside Italy">

    Put their country in `tax_id`, with their VAT number as `code` if they have one. A private individual like the one below has none, so `tax_id` holds only the country. Leave out inboxes: SDI can't deliver outside Italy, so the file always uses the cross-border recipient code `XXXXXXX`, and you send the customer a copy yourself.
