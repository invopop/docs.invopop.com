  <Accordion title="Italian business">

    The `tax_id` carries the *partita IVA*, and the `it-sdi-code` inbox carries the *codice destinatario* SDI routes by. A customer who receives through certified email instead has an `it-sdi-pec` inbox holding the address. A customer who has both can carry both: the invoice routes by the *codice destinatario*, and the PEC is left out of the file.

  <Accordion title="Italian private individual">

    The *codice fiscale* identifies them, as an `identities` entry keyed `it-fiscal-code`. They have no VAT number, so the `tax_id` holds only the country, and there's no inbox. SDI doesn't deliver to private individuals — it files the invoice in their personal area on the tax authority portal — so you must give the customer a copy yourself, normally as a PDF.

  <Accordion title="Customer outside Italy">

    The country in `tax_id` is all SDI needs, alongside a VAT number for a business inside the EU. No inbox: SDI cannot deliver outside Italy, so the file always carries the cross-border recipient code `XXXXXXX`, and an inbox on the party is ignored.
