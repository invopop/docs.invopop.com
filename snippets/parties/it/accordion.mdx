  <Accordion title="Italian private individual">

    The *codice fiscale* identifies them, as an `identities` entry keyed `it-fiscal-code`. There's no `tax_id` and no inbox. SDI doesn't deliver to private individuals — it files the invoice in their personal area on the tax authority portal — so you must give the customer a copy yourself, normally as a PDF.
