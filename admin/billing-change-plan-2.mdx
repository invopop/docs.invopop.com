You can track your pop usage by checking the progress bar in your billing account.

<Frame>
  ![Pops usage progress bar in the billing account](/assets/admin/billing-pops-usage.png "Pops usage")
</Frame>

To see the pop consumption linked to the Seats you are using, click on the live Billing Account. In the **Seats** section you will find the list of apps, the seat count each has, as well as the associated pops.

<Frame>
  ![Seats section listing apps with seat counts and pops](/assets/admin/billing-seats-pops.png "Seats and pops")
</Frame>

## FAQ

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="What happens if I run out of pops in my live billing account?">
    To continue using Invopop you will need to upgrade your plan to the next pop tier.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="How do merchants (Seats) contribute to my pop count?">
    Technically, a **Seat** corresponds to a Tax ID registered to issue invoices in a specific network. Every account needs at least one.

    In practice, most companies need only one seat or a handful. Some, however, use Invopop to provide e-invoicing to their own customers: each customer registered counts as a seat.

    Every seat consumes pops from your monthly allowance: 100 pops are deducted the moment you register it, and 100 pops again every 30 days from that registration date for as long as it stays registered. 
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="What happens if I run out of pops in my sandbox billing account?">
    Get in touch with us at [support@invopop.com](mailto:support@invopop.com), and unless we detect abnormal usage, we will extend your sandbox billing account with additional pops.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
