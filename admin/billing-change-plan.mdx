To modify or cancel your subscription, navigate to **Billing,** go to the **Subscription** section, and click on the three dots on the right. 

<Frame>
  ![Subscription menu with Modify and Cancel subscription options](/assets/admin/subscription-modify-menu.png "Modify or cancel subscription")
</Frame>

Once you click <kbd>Modify</kbd> , you will be able to remove or add products to your subscription.

<Frame>
  ![Modify subscription products](/assets/admin/subscription-modify-products.png "Modify subscription")
</Frame>
