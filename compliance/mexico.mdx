## Executive summary

Every taxpayer in Mexico must issue electronic invoices. The invoice is an XML document called **CFDI** (Comprobante Fiscal Digital por Internet). The tax agency is the **SAT** (Servicio de Administración Tributaria).

Mexico uses a clearance model. An authorized certification provider, called a **PAC**, validates and stamps each CFDI before the invoice has fiscal effect. The PAC sends a copy to the SAT at the same moment. The same rules apply to business (B2B), consumer (B2C) and public sector (B2G) sales.

| | |
|---|---|
| **Scope** | B2B, B2C, B2G |
| **Format** | CFDI 4.0 (XML) |
| **Model** | Clearance: a PAC stamps each document before it is valid |
| **Agency** | [SAT](https://sat.gob.mx) |
| **Invopop app** | [SAT Mexico](/apps/sat-mexico) |
| **PAC** | SW Sapien, through the [SW Sapien app](/apps/sw-sapien) |
| **Archive period** | 5 years |

This page tells you what the country requires. It does not show fields or codes. When you are ready to build, go to [Implement Mexico](#implement-mexico).

## How invoicing works

<Steps>
  <Step title="The supplier prepares the invoice">
    The supplier's system sends the invoice data to Invopop as a [GOBL](https://docs.gobl.org) document.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Invopop builds and seals the CFDI">
    Invopop converts the document to CFDI 4.0 and seals it with the supplier's digital seal certificate.
  </Step>
  <Step title="The PAC stamps the CFDI">
    The PAC validates the CFDI and adds the fiscal folio (a UUID) and the SAT seal. The PAC rejects a CFDI that is more than 72 hours old.
  </Step>
  <Step title="The SAT receives a copy">
    The PAC sends the stamped CFDI to the SAT. No separate report is necessary.
  </Step>
  <Step title="The customer receives the invoice">
    The supplier gives the customer the stamped XML. A PDF is a printed copy of the XML, and the XML is the legal document.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## What a taxpayer needs

A supplier must have these items before it can issue a CFDI:

| Item | What it is | Who provides it |
|---|---|---|
| **RFC** | The Mexican tax ID. Companies have 12 characters and individuals have 13. | SAT |
| **e.firma** | The advanced electronic signature of the taxpayer. It signs the PAC authorization. | SAT |
| **CSD** | The digital seal certificate. It seals each CFDI. | SAT, requested with the e.firma |
| **PAC authorization** | A signed manifest that lets the PAC send CFDI copies to the SAT. | The supplier signs it with the e.firma |

Invopop does not create a CSD. The supplier uploads its CSD during registration. See the [supplier registration guide](/guides/mx-sat-supplier).

The supplier also needs four data points from each Mexican customer: RFC, legal name, tax regime and postal code. The PAC compares these with the SAT registry and rejects the invoice if one of them is different. See [Parties](/guides/mx-cfdi-parties).

## Document types

A CFDI has one of five types. A sixth document, for withholdings, has its own format.

| Type | Use | Invopop | Build it |
|---|---|:---:|---|
| **Ingreso** | Sales invoice | Yes | [Invoice](/guides/mx-cfdi-invoice) |
| **Egreso** | Credit note for a return, discount or correction | Yes | [Credit note](/guides/mx-cfdi-credit-note) |
| **Pago** | Payment receipt for an invoice that the customer pays later | Yes | [Payment receipt](/guides/mx-cfdi-payment) |
| **Traslado** | Movement of goods without a sale | No | |
| **Nómina** | Payroll receipt | No | |
| **Retenciones** | Withholding certificate | No | |

Some sectors must add a **complemento** to the CFDI. A complemento is an extra block of data, for example Carta Porte for freight or Comercio Exterior for exports. The list below shows which ones Invopop supports.

## Decisions that change your integration

### Does the customer pay now or later?

Each invoice says if the customer pays in full in the month of issue, or later.

- **Paid in full in the month of issue.** The invoice is the only document.
- **Paid later or in installments.** The supplier must also issue a payment receipt for each payment. The receipt is due by day 5 of the month after the payment.

If an invoice says "paid in full" and the payment does not arrive in that month, the supplier must cancel it and issue it again as "paid later". Select "paid later" when you are not sure. See [how Invopop sets the payment fields](/guides/mx-cfdi-invoice#payment-method-and-means).

### Do you sell to consumers?

A consumer who does not ask for an invoice does not get an individual CFDI. The supplier adds these sales together in a **global invoice** for a day, a week, a month or two months. The global invoice is due 24 hours after the period closes. See [Global invoice](/guides/mx-cfdi-global).

### How do you correct a mistake?

A stamped CFDI cannot change. The supplier has two options:

- Issue a **credit note** to decrease the amount of an invoice.
- **Cancel** the CFDI at the SAT, with or without a replacement.

For most B2B invoices, the customer must accept the cancellation. The customer has three business days to answer, and no answer means acceptance. See the [cancellation guide](/guides/mx-sat-cancelling).

### Do you receive supplier invoices?

Invopop can download the CFDI that a taxpayer receives from the SAT and convert them to GOBL. This needs the e.firma of the taxpayer. See the [receiving guide](/guides/mx-sat-receiving).

## Time limits

| Subject | Limit |
|---|---|
| Stamp | Not later than 72 hours after the issue date and time of the CFDI |
| Global invoice | 24 hours after the close of the period |
| Payment receipt | Day 5 of the month after the payment |
| Cancellation | The month in which the annual income tax return for the year of issue is due |
| Customer answer to a cancellation | Three business days |

## Legal requirements

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Archive period">
    Taxpayers must keep their accounting records, which include CFDIs, for five years (Article 30 of the Federal Tax Code). The period starts on the date the related tax return was filed, or was due.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Penalties">
    It is an infraction not to issue a CFDI, not to give it to the customer, or to issue one without the legal content (Articles 83 and 84 of the Federal Tax Code). The general fine is MXN 22,300 to 127,530. For a repeat infraction, the authority can also close the premises for 3 to 15 days.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Certification provider (PAC)">
    A PAC is a private company that the SAT authorizes to validate and stamp CFDIs. Invopop is not a PAC. Invopop uses SW Sapien as the PAC, and the supplier does not need a contract with SW Sapien.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Suspect supplier list (Article 69-B)">
    The SAT publishes a list of taxpayers that it presumes issue invoices for operations that did not occur. A buyer can lose the deduction for an invoice from a listed supplier. Many companies check their suppliers against this list. Invopop does not do this check.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Digital platforms">
    Digital platforms that operate in Mexico have a separate duty to give the SAT access to their operations data. This duty is not part of the CFDI flow. See the [digital platform reporting guide](/guides/mx-sat-platform-reporting).
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
