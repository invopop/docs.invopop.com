		[RO e-Factura](https://mfinante.gov.ro/ro/web/efactura) is Romania's mandatory national e-invoicing system, run by the Ministry of Finance and the tax authority, [ANAF](https://www.anaf.ro) (_Agenția Națională de Administrare Fiscală_). Businesses reach it through the API or through SPV (_Spațiul Privat Virtual_), ANAF's online portal.

		Electronic invoicing in Romania operates on a clearance model: every B2B invoice must be uploaded to RO e-Factura within 5 working days of issue, and B2C invoices are reported the same way. The platform validates each invoice, seals it with the Ministry of Finance signature and makes it available to the buyer in SPV. The sealed XML is the legal invoice.

		Invoices are encoded in UBL 2.1 following CIUS-RO, the Romanian profile of EN 16931. Invopop authorizes on behalf of each party through ANAF's OAuth flow, so the party's digital certificate never leaves its owner.

	**Key features**

	* **Workflow automation:** Automated workflows for registering parties, sending invoices, and importing received invoices through RO e-Factura.
	* **Party registration:** Register a party with its Romanian tax ID (CIF). The certificate holder authorizes Invopop once on ANAF's site, from a link stored in the party's meta.
	* **Authorization renewal:** ANAF authorizations last one year. Invopop renews access in the background during that year. Notifications before the party has to register again are in development.
	* **CIUS-RO XML generation:** Compliant UBL 2.1 generation for invoices, credit notes, corrective, self-billed and accounting invoices, checked against the official ANAF schematron before upload.
	* **Clearance tracking:** Invopop waits for ANAF's decision on each upload and attaches the official XML returned by ANAF to the invoice.
	* **Automatic routing:** B2B, B2C, self-billed and enforcement uploads are picked from the invoice itself, with no configuration.
	* **Invoice import & sync:** Periodically fetch invoices received in SPV and import them into Invopop in GOBL format.

	<Note>
	  **Sandbox and live environments**

	  Invopop sandbox workspaces send every invoice to ANAF's test environment, where documents have no fiscal value. Live workspaces send to ANAF's production environment.
	</Note>
