		The [DIAN](https://www.dian.gov.co/) in Colombia stands for _Dirección de Impuestos y Aduanas Nacionales_ (National Directorate of Taxes and Customs). It is the government agency responsible for managing and overseeing tax collection, customs operations, and facilitating international trade.

		Electronic invoicing in Colombia is mandatory and operates on a real-time clearance model whereby every invoice must receive a unique identifier called a CUFE (_Código Único de Factura Electrónica_) issued by the DIAN; credit and debit notes receive the equivalent CUDE.

		Invopop's **Colombia app** has partnered with [Alegra](https://www.alegra.com), an authorized technology provider for DIAN electronic invoicing, to onboard suppliers and issue documents on their behalf. The app handles the DIAN enablement (_habilitación_) through a hosted portal, keeps track of the supplier's billing numbering resolutions, and clears invoices, credit notes, and debit notes in real time.

		**Key features**

		* **Workflow automation:** Automated workflows for registering suppliers and issuing invoices, credit notes, and debit notes through the DIAN system.
		* **Clearance:** Every document is validated by the DIAN before it's issued, receiving its unique CUFE or CUDE code as the `dian-cude` stamp on the envelope.
		* **Supplier onboarding:** A hosted portal walks suppliers through the DIAN enablement test set, with the workflow resuming automatically once the DIAN accepts it. You can also [submit the test set via the API](/api-ref/apps/gov-co/enablement-submit).
		* **Resolution management:** Billing numbering resolutions linked to Alegra are fetched from the DIAN automatically, and invoice numbers are validated against them before sending. You can also [list them via the API](/api-ref/apps/gov-co/resolutions-list).
		* **UBL 2.1 generation:** The signed DIAN XML (AttachedDocument) is stored as a file on the silo entry.
		* **QR code:** The DIAN QR code is added as the `dian-qr` stamp, ready for legally compliant PDF generation.
		* **Legally compliant PDF invoices:** The send workflow renders a PDF with all mandatory fields, the CUFE or CUDE, and the DIAN QR code.
