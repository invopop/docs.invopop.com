		Germany has no central e-invoicing platform. Businesses exchange **XRechnung** and **ZUGFeRD** invoices directly with their customers, by email or through the [Peppol](/apps/peppol) network.

		Invopop's **Germany app** adds the steps that German suppliers and invoices need. It approves a supplier before its Peppol registration, and it builds ZUGFeRD PDFs. The **Export document** step, available in every workspace, converts the invoices to XRechnung or ZUGFeRD.

		**Key features**

		* **Email or Peppol:** send invoices with the [Email](/apps/email) app and receive them with [Connect](/apps/connect), or register the supplier on Peppol.
		* **Supplier approval:** the supplier's representative completes an onboarding with an ID document and a proof of ownership, before Invopop registers the supplier on Peppol.
		* **Peppol ID:** registration adds the supplier's GEBA (scheme `0246`), the default Peppol identifier for German businesses.
		* **ZUGFeRD PDFs:** embed the exported ZUGFeRD XML into the invoice PDF.
		* **ZUGFeRD import:** create an invoice from a ZUGFeRD PDF that you received.

	<Tab title="Actions">
		The following workflow actions will be available once you install and enable this app.

		Registration
		<Card title="Register supplier for approval" icon="https://assets.invopop.com/flags/de.svg" horizontal>
			Add the GEBA Peppol inbox to a party without one, and publish the onboarding link that the supplier follows.
		</Card>
		<Card title="Wait for supplier approval" icon="https://assets.invopop.com/flags/de.svg" horizontal>
			Wait for the supplier to complete the onboarding. Once it is approved, store the approval on the party.
		</Card>
		<Card title="Unregister supplier" icon="https://assets.invopop.com/flags/de.svg" horizontal>
			Cancel an open onboarding and remove the supplier's approval. A new registration starts a new onboarding.
		</Card>

		Documents
		<Card title="Embed XML into PDF" icon="https://assets.invopop.com/flags/de.svg" horizontal>
			Embed the ZUGFeRD or XRechnung CII XML that the **Export document** step stored into the invoice PDF, producing a PDF/A-3.
		</Card>
		<Card title="Import ZUGFeRD PDF" icon="https://assets.invopop.com/flags/de.svg" horizontal>
			Create an invoice from the ZUGFeRD PDF at the URL in the job's `url` argument, and store the PDF on the invoice. To receive invoices by email, use [Connect](/apps/connect).
		</Card>
